# THE WORLD — R&D Portfolio P0 Evolution

## Decision
Portfolio conversion is the **P0 research priority** for THE WORLD R&D.

The objective is not to maximize research volume. The objective is to increase the rate at which research becomes a human-inspectable, evidence-backed artifact that can be understood, evaluated, reused, sold, or operated.

## Daily loop
1. **OBSERVE** the canonical `PORTFOLIO.md`.
2. **RANK** proof gaps, customer-value potential and distance to `VERIFIED`.
3. **CHOOSE ONE** primary portfolio bet for the day.
4. **FORM** a falsifiable hypothesis and explicit counterevidence target.
5. **SENJU** receives only a bounded technical directive (`robustness`, `learning`, `balance`, `efficiency`).
6. Existing workers **BUILD / VERIFY** the smallest material improvement.
7. **PORTFOLIO GATE** rejects code, PRs and internal logs as the artifact itself.
8. **SLACK** receives the material delta in human language.
9. **LEARN** from proof, failure and dissent; feed the next hypothesis back into R&D.

## Efficiency rules
- One material primary bet per day beats many shallow tasks.
- Do not spawn a new agent when an existing worker can own the work.
- Reuse the existing R&D x Senju bridge and governance chain.
- A verified no-op is better than noisy fake progress.
- Repeated failure becomes an explicit research question, not a hidden retry loop.
- Slack receives outcomes, evidence gaps and next actions; raw logs stay out.

## Portfolio gate
A portfolio entry must remain consistent with `PORTFOLIO_CONTRACT.md`:
- human-inspectable artifact required;
- `VERIFIED` requires access plus evidence of the claimed core behavior;
- source code, a PR, a commit, an issue or a log dump alone is not a portfolio artifact;
- technical evidence may support proof but cannot invent market evidence.

## Senju boundary
Senju is a technical improvement engine, not a market oracle.

It may improve bounded technical evidence and strategy quality. It may not create or infer willingness-to-pay, market validation, contract value, payments or revenue from simulator scores.

## Daily evidence package
The workflow stores for 30 days:
- selected portfolio bet;
- ranking reasons;
- bounded Senju directive;
- candidate Senju strategy;
- Senju directive audit;
- Slack-ready daily summary.

## Primary metrics
Track improvement in:
- BUILDING/EXPERIMENT -> VERIFIED conversion;
- human-inspectable artifact ratio;
- behavioral-proof completeness;
- days since last material portfolio delta;
- R&D hypothesis -> artifact conversion;
- repeated-no-op and repeated-failure rate;
- owner effort required to understand the result.
