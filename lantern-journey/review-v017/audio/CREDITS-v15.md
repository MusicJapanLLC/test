# v15 audio

Original character reaction performances (four) and combat sound effects (six) generated with Runway. Reactions use expressive Eleven v3 presets Brodie / Pip, language ja. Effects use eleven_text_to_sound_v2, nonverbal sound design. Source prompts, generation IDs and measured levels are in voice-v15-manifest.json and sfx-v15-manifest.json.

Edited: silence trimming, fades, peak normalisation, mono resampling, MP3/OGG encoding. Sword, bow and magic alternate takes are pitch/level variants of the generated masters. No third-party character voices or music were sampled. Existing music and other sound credits remain in CREDITS-v14.md.
