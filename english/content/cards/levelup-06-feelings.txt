//! Feelings, Said Better | Name what you feel without sounding like a textbook | levelup

# I'm torn.
@ talk | feelings, friends | real | deep | 3
= 迷ってる（気持ちが二つに引き裂かれてる）
: Means you can't decide between two choices.
A: Are you taking the Tokyo job?
B: I'm torn. The money's better, but my whole life is in Osaka.
- I'm torn between staying in the band and going solo.
✗ I am confused which to choose. | textbook | confused は「混乱」で意味がずれる
✓ I'm on the fence. | 決めかねてる。もう少し軽い
? torn ＝tear（引き裂く）の過去分詞。気持ちが二つに引き裂かれている。between A and B で何と何で迷っているかを言える。
! ただの「迷う」より感情的な葛藤がある。大事な決断の話で使う。

# It's been weighing on me.
@ talk | feelings, friends | real | deep | 2
= ずっと気になってる（心に重くのしかかってる）
: Something has been worrying you for a while.
A: You've been quiet lately. What's up?
B: I still owe my brother money, and it's been weighing on me.
- Honestly, that conversation has been weighing on me all week.
✗ I am worrying about it heavily. | textbook | 不自然な直訳
? weigh on ＝重さが上にかかる。現在完了進行形で「最近ずっと」の継続を表す。悩みを打ち明けるときの自然な入り方。
! 言われたら Do you want to talk about it? と聞いてあげると優しい。

# I'm not gonna lie,
@ talk | feelings, friends, dm | real | chill | 3
= 正直言うと（ぶっちゃけ）
: Introduces an honest feeling or opinion.
A: How was the date?
B: I'm not gonna lie, I was super nervous.
- Not gonna lie, that sermon hit me hard today.
- I'm not gonna lie, I almost cried.
✗ To tell the truth, | textbook | 間違いではないけど古くて硬い
? 本音を言う前の前置き。感情を素直に言うのが恥ずかしいときのクッションになる。テキストでは ngl と略す。I'm を省いて Not gonna lie, とも。
! 悪口の前置きにも使えるけど、気持ちを打ち明けるときに使うと一気にネイティブっぽい。
