// Textbook vs Real. Things we learned at school that make native speakers smile (or wince).

# I'm fine, thank you. And you?
@ talk | daily | textbook | chill | 3
= 元気です、ありがとう。あなたは？
- How are you? — I'm fine, thank you. And you?
✓ Good, you? | 一番自然
✓ Pretty good. How about you? | 少し丁寧
✓ Not bad! | 軽く
! 日本の教科書の象徴。間違いではないけどロボットっぽく聞こえる。
