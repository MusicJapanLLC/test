//! Softening & Hedging | Say it without sounding blunt | levelup

# I might be wrong, but
@ talk | work, friends | real | worksafe, warm | 3
= 間違ってたらごめんだけど
: Softens a statement by admitting you could be wrong.
A: The show starts at eight, right?
B: I might be wrong, but I think doors open at eight and the show's at nine.
A: Oh, good catch.
- I might be wrong, but didn't we already pay for this?
✗ Maybe I am wrong, but | textbook | 文法はOKでも硬くて不自然
✓ I could be wrong, but | ほぼ同じ意味。どちらも自然
? 自分の意見の前に「保険」をかけるクッション。言い切ると強すぎる場面で、相手が訂正しやすい空気を作れる。実はけっこう自信があるときにも使うのがネイティブ流。
! 日本語の「間違ってたらすみません」に近いけど、謝る感じはない。sorry は付けなくていい。

# Not to be that guy, but
@ talk | friends, work | real | funny, chill | 3
= うるさいやつになりたくないけど
: Used before a small complaint or correction so you don't sound annoying.
A: Not to be that guy, but you still owe me for the ramen.
B: Ugh, I know. Sending it now.
- Not to be that guy, but the meeting was at ten, not ten thirty.
- Not to be that guy, but can we turn the music down a little?
✗ I am sorry to say this, but | textbook | 重すぎて深刻な話に聞こえる
✓ Not to be annoying, but | 同じ意味、少しストレート
? that guy ＝「みんなが嫌がるタイプの人」（細かい・空気読まない人）。自分で先にネタにすることで、細かい指摘でも角が立たない。女性なら Not to be that girl, but も使う。
! 言ったあとに結局その「細かい指摘」をするのがお約束。ちょっと笑いが起きるくらいの軽いフレーズ。

# I'm not complaining, but
@ talk | work, friends, food | real | funny, chill | 2
= 文句じゃないんだけど
: Softens a complaint, often with humor.
A: I'm not complaining, but this is the third time we've had curry this week.
B: You love curry.
A: I do. I'm just saying.
- I'm not complaining, but the AC has been off for an hour.
✗ I have a complaint. | textbook | クレームっぽく重い
✓ Not to complain, but | 同じ意味
? 「文句じゃないけど」と言いながら、だいたい文句を言うのがお約束。軽い不満をユーモアで伝えるクッション。本気のクレームには使わない。

# Don't take this the wrong way, but
@ talk | friends, work, conflict | real | warm, worksafe | 3
= 悪く取らないでほしいんだけど
: Asks someone not to feel hurt by what comes next.
A: Don't take this the wrong way, but you seem really tired lately.
B: Yeah. The night shifts are catching up with me.
A: Maybe take a day off?
✗ Please understand my true meaning. | textbook | 意味が伝わらず重い
✓ I say this with love, but | 親しい相手への冗談まじりの言い方
? the wrong way ＝「悪い意味で」。相手を心配しての指摘や、言いにくいフィードバックの前置き。No offense よりずっと丁寧で、仕事でも使える。

# With all due respect,
@ talk | work, conflict, business | real | cold, worksafe | 2
= お言葉ですが
: A formal cushion before disagreeing, which often sounds sharp.
A: We should just copy what the competition is doing.
B: With all due respect, that's how we got into this mess.
- With all due respect, you haven't seen the numbers.
✗ With all due respect, you're an idiot. | cringe | ドラマでよくあるギャグ。リアルで言うと大惨事
✓ I see it a little differently. | 本当にやわらかく反対したいとき
? 直訳は「十分な敬意を払った上で」。でも実際は「これから反論します」の合図で、かなりトゲがある。日本語の「お言葉ですが」とほぼ同じ温度感。
! 上司に使うと「喧嘩売ってる」と受け取られることも。やわらかくしたいなら I might be wrong, but の方が無難。

# I don't want to overstep, but
@ talk | work, friends, business | real | warm, worksafe | 2
= 出しゃばるつもりはないんだけど
: Used before giving advice that isn't really your place to give.

✗ I'm sorry for being rude, but | textbook | 失礼と決めつけて謝ってしまう
✓ Feel free to ignore me, but | もっとカジュアル
? overstep ＝「（立場の）線を越える」。頼まれてないアドバイスや、人の家庭・お金の話に口を出すときの丁寧なクッション。後輩から先輩にも使える。

# This might be a dumb question, but
@ talk | work, study, business | real | warm, worksafe | 3
= バカな質問かもしれないけど

✓ Quick question, probably a dumb one. | もっと軽い言い方
? 質問のハードルを下げる定番。言われた側は Not dumb at all. / There are no dumb questions. と返すのがお約束。学習者が使うと聞き返しやすくなって会話がラクになる。
! 日本人は謝りすぎがち。sorry より this might be a dumb question の方が前向きに聞こえる。

# If you don't mind me asking,
@ talk | friends, business, pickup | real | warm, worksafe | 3
= もし差し支えなければ（聞いてもいい？）
: Softens a personal or sensitive question.

B: Around six hundred bucks. Worth every penny.

✗ Can I ask a private question? | textbook | 身構えさせてしまう
✓ If that's not too personal, | 年齢や恋愛の話のとき
? 値段・年齢・仕事・恋愛など、ちょっと踏み込んだ質問の前後に付ける。文の最後に付けても自然。mind me asking と me が入るのが口語。
! アメリカでも給料や家賃を直接聞くのはややデリケート。このクッションがあるだけで印象が全然違う。

# If anything,
@ talk | friends, work, health | real | chill | 2
= むしろ
: Suggests that the opposite is more likely true.
A: Did you gain weight on your trip?
B: If anything, I lost weight. I walked everywhere.
- It's not too spicy. If anything, it needs more heat.
- If anything, the break made my voice stronger.
✗ Rather, I lost weight. | textbook | Rather 単独で文頭は硬い
✓ Actually, I lost weight. | よりシンプル
? 相手の予想を「むしろ逆」とやんわり訂正する言い方。いきなり No から入らないので角が立たない。「どちらかと言えば」の感覚でも使える。

# It's not that..., it's just that
@ talk | crush, friends, conflict | real | deep, warm | 3
= 〜ってわけじゃなくて、ただ〜なだけ
: Rejects a harsh reason and gives the real, softer one.
A: You don't like my cooking?
B: It's not that I don't like it, it's just that I'm full.
- It's not that I'm mad. It's just that you didn't call.
- It's not that the song's bad, it's just that it's too long.
✗ I don't dislike it, but | textbook | 二重否定で分かりにくい
✓ It's not so much the price as the timing. | ちょっと上級の同じ型
? 相手が想像している「キツい理由」を先に否定してから、本当の理由を出す型。気持ちを説明するときの超定番。It's not that のあとは文（I don't like it など）が来る。

# Ideally,
@ talk | work, business, money | real | worksafe | 3
