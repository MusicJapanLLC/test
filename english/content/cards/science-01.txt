# the spacing effect
@ science | study | real | deep | 2
= 分散効果（間隔をあけて復習すると覚えやすい）
- Don't cram. Spread it out. It's called the spacing effect.
- Reviewing a word tomorrow, then next week, beats ten times tonight.
! 同じ回数でも、一夜漬けより間をあけて復習した方が長く残る。このアプリの「Review」はこの仕組みで出題している。
^ Cepeda, N. J., et al. (2006). Distributed practice in verbal recall tasks: A review and quantitative synthesis. Psychological Bulletin, 132(3), 354–380. | https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Distributed+practice+in+verbal+recall+tasks+a+review+and+quantitative+synthesis

@ science | study | real | deep | 2
= 想起練習（思い出す練習で記憶が強くなる）
- Rereading feels productive, but testing yourself works better. That's retrieval practice.
- Close the book and try to remember. It's supposed to feel hard.
! 読み返すより「思い出そうとする」方が記憶に残る（テスト効果）。フィードの途中にクイズが出るのはこのため。

# chunking
@ science | study | real | deep | 3
= チャンク化（まとまりで覚える）
- Phone numbers are easier to remember in chunks: 090, 1234, 5678.
- Learn "I'm gonna head out" as one chunk, not five words.
