//! Storytelling | Tell a story people actually listen to | levelup

# So get this.
@ talk | friends, work, daily | real | funny, chill | 3
= ちょっと聞いてよ（すごい話があって）
: Grabs attention before a surprising story.
A: So get this. My landlord wants to raise the rent and paint my door pink.
B: Pink? Why?
A: Nobody knows.
- So get this: the client paid early.
✗ Please listen to my story. | textbook | 丁寧すぎて逆に退屈そう
✓ Get this. | So を省いた短い版
? get this ＝「これ、分かる？／聞いて」。驚く話や信じられない話の前に付けて、相手の注意を一気に引く。チャットでは Get this: のようにコロンで本題につなぐ。

# You're not gonna believe this.
@ talk | friends, dm, work | real | funny, warm | 3
= 信じられない話なんだけど
: Builds excitement before big news.
A: You're not gonna believe this.
B: What? Tell me.
A: The label wants to sign us.
- You're not gonna believe who I just ran into.
✗ This is an unbelievable story. | textbook | 説明的で盛り上がらない
✓ You'll never guess what happened. | 同じ温度感
? 話の前に相手の期待を上げる定番の振り。who / what / where を続けて You're not gonna believe who I saw. のようにも使える。声のテンションを上げるのがコツ。

# Guess what?
@ talk | friends, crush, dm | real | warm, funny | 3
= ねえ聞いて！／なんだと思う？
: Opens a conversation with exciting news.
A: Guess what?
B: What?
A: I passed the interview!
- Guess what? I finally finished the album.
✗ Please guess. | textbook | クイズみたいで不自然
✓ Guess who I saw today? | 人の話のとき
? 本当に当ててほしいわけではなく、「ニュースがあるよ」の合図。返しは What? が定番。Guess what I found / Guess who called のように後ろに続けることもできる。
! 子どもっぽいかなと思うかもしれないけど、大人も普通に使う。嬉しいニュースのときにぴったり。

# Did I ever tell you about
@ talk | friends, bar, crush | real | warm, chill | 3
= 〜の話ってしたっけ？
: Opens a story you might have told before.
A: Did I ever tell you about the time I got locked out in Seoul?
B: No! What happened?
- Did I ever tell you about my first gig?
- Stop me if I've told you this before.
✗ I will tell you a story. | textbook | 先生の朗読みたい
✓ Have I told you about | ほぼ同じ意味
? 話を始める自然な入り方。the time I 〜（〜したときのこと）とセットで使うことが多い。前に話したかも、というときは Stop me if I've told you this before. と付けると親切。

# This one time,
@ talk | friends, bar, travel | real | funny, chill | 3
= 前にさ、こんなことがあって
: Starts a story about a specific past event.
A: This one time, I fell asleep on the last train and woke up in Nara.
B: No way. What did you do?
A: Walked around with the deer until morning.
- This one time, a customer paid for a latte in ten-yen coins.
✗ One day in the past, | textbook | 童話っぽい
✓ One time, | this なしでも同じ
? 過去のエピソードを話し始めるときの一番カジュアルな言い方。Once upon a time は昔話専用なので、普通の会話では使わない。

# So what happened was
@ talk | friends, work, conflict | real | chill | 3
= えっとね、何があったかっていうと
: Starts explaining a situation from the beginning.
A: Why are you two not talking?
B: So what happened was, he borrowed my amp and never gave it back.
- So what happened was, the train stopped for an hour.
- Okay, so what happened was, I forgot the password.
✗ The thing that happened is this. | textbook | 不自然に硬い
✓ So basically, | もっとざっくり説明するとき
? 経緯や言い訳を説明するときの入り方。文法的には what happened was 〜 で「起きたことは〜」。ネイティブは was のあとに少し間を置いてから話す。

# Picture this:
@ talk | friends, business, music | real | funny, warm | 2
= 想像してみて：
: Asks the listener to imagine a scene.
A: Picture this: rooftop show, sunset, the whole city behind you.
B: Okay, I'm in.
- Picture this: me, in a suit, at the gym.
- Picture this: you wake up, and the coffee's already made.
✗ Please imagine this situation. | textbook | 説明会みたい
✓ Imagine this: | 同じ意味
? 聞き手の頭に映像を作らせる語り出し。話を盛り上げたり、提案を魅力的に見せたりするのに使える。名詞を並べるだけ（rooftop, sunset...）でも成立するのがポイント。

# So there I was,
@ talk | friends, bar, travel | real | funny | 2
= で、気づけばこんな状況でさ
: Sets the scene at the key moment of a story.
A: So there I was, on stage, and my guitar strap snaps.
B: Oh no.
A: Right in the middle of the solo.
- So there I was, locked out, in my pajamas, at three in the morning.
✗ At that time I was there. | textbook | 意味がぼやける
✓ And there I am, | 現在形でさらに臨場感
? 物語の山場の直前に「その場面」へ聞き手を連れていく言い方。続く文を現在形（my strap snaps）にすると、まるで今起きているような臨場感が出る。英語の語りでよく使うテクニック。

? suddenly は文章っぽく、会話では all of a sudden の方がよく聞く。話のリズムを作るために、直前で少し間を置くと効果的。

# Out of nowhere,
@ talk | friends, travel, crush | real | chill, funny | 3
= どこからともなく／いきなり
: Says something appeared or happened unexpectedly.
A: Out of nowhere, this guy walks up and asks if I'm the singer.
B: Were you?

? 予想外の人・物・出来事が急に現れたときに使う。came out of nowhere の形でもよく使う。out of the blue は特に「連絡や話が突然」に使うことが多い。

# Next thing I know,
@ talk | friends, bar, travel | real | funny | 3
= 気づいたら〜してた
: Jumps to a surprising result in a story.
A: I said yes to one drink. Next thing I know, it's 5 a.m. and I'm singing at karaoke.
B: Classic.
- I closed my eyes for a second. Next thing I know, we're in Kyoto.
- Next thing I know, I'm signing a two-year contract.
✗ After that, suddenly I found that | textbook | 長くて話がもたつく
✓ Next thing you know, | 一般論や予測にも使う
? 途中経過をすっ飛ばして「気づいたらこうなってた」と展開を早める言い方。後ろは現在形（it's 5 a.m.）にするとテンポが良くなる。

- Before I knew it, I was crying in the car.

✓ In no time, | 「あっという間に」
? 時間の速さや、自分でも気づかないうちに何かが起きたことを表す。Next thing I know より少し落ち着いた語り口で、しんみりした話にも使える。

# And that's when
@ talk | friends, crush, work | real | deep, funny | 3

: Marks the turning point of a story.
A: We were packing up after the show. And that's when I saw her.

A: The producer from the label.

✓ That's when it hit me. | 「その時気づいた」
? 話の山場・転換点を示す合図。that's when のあとに出来事や気づき（I realized / it hit me）が来る。声を少し落として間を置くと効果的。

# Little did I know,

# Sure enough,

# As luck would have it,

# Just my luck.

# Of all people,
