// Travel, housing & paperwork

visa | n | ビザ | permission to enter a country | I need a working holiday visa. || My visa runs out in March. || How long does the visa process take? | travel | apply for a visa / get a visa。期限切れは My visa runs out / expires.
boarding pass | n | 搭乗券 | ticket to board a plane | Here's my boarding pass. || I have my boarding pass on my phone. || Have your boarding pass and ID ready. | travel | 搭乗券は ticket ではなく boarding pass。スマホの mobile boarding pass も普通。
gate | n | 搭乗口 | where you board a plane | We're at gate 32. || The gate changed. We have to run. || Boarding starts at the gate in twenty minutes. | travel | gate change（搭乗口変更）は空港アナウンスの頻出語。
layover | n | 乗り継ぎ | a stop between flights | I have a three-hour layover in Seoul. || A long layover means airport ramen. || Is there a layover, or is it a direct flight? | travel | 乗り継ぎ待ちの時間のこと。直行便は direct flight / nonstop。
delay | n | 遅延 | waiting longer than planned | There's a two-hour delay. || Sorry for the delay. The espresso machine broke. || The train was delayed, so I was late for my shift. | travel | 動詞で be delayed（遅れる）も頻出。Sorry for the delay. は返信が遅れたときにも使う。
luggage | n | 荷物 | bags for travel | My luggage didn't arrive. || How much luggage are you bringing? || I travel with one bag. No checked luggage. | travel | 数えない名詞。a luggage ではなく a piece of luggage / one bag と言う。
round trip | n | 往復 | going and coming back | A round trip to Sydney is pricey. || Is that price one-way or round trip? || A round trip to Tokyo by bullet train adds up fast. | travel | アメリカは round trip、イギリスは return。round-trip ticket の形も。
one-way | adj | 片道の | going only one direction | Just a one-way ticket. || It's a one-way street, so turn left here. || I bought a one-way ticket and figured out the rest later. | travel | 片道切符のほか one-way street（一方通行）にも使う。
customs | n | 税関 | checking goods at the border | We went through customs. || Customs took forever this time. || Do I need to declare this at customs? | travel | 税関は必ず s 付き。単数の custom は「習慣」。
souvenir | n | お土産 | something to remember a trip | I bought souvenirs for my family. || I always bring Osaka snacks as souvenirs. || This scar is my souvenir from that trip. | travel | 自分用の記念品の意味が強い。職場に配るお土産は gift と言うほうが自然なことも。
sightseeing | n | 観光 | visiting famous places | We spent the day sightseeing. || I'm not here for sightseeing. I'm here for the food. || Let's do some sightseeing before the show. | travel | go sightseeing / do some sightseeing の形。sightseeing tour も定番。
tourist trap | n | 観光客向けのぼったくり場所 | an overpriced place for tourists | That street is a tourist trap. || Skip the tourist traps and eat where the locals eat. || That cafe is a tourist trap. Eight bucks for drip coffee. | travel | 高くてイマイチな観光客向けの場所。悪口なので店の人の前では言わない。
local | n | 地元の人 | someone who lives in the area | Ask a local where to eat. || Only locals know about this bar. || Are you a local, or just visiting? | travel | 名詞で「地元の人」。形容詞で my local bar（行きつけの近所の店）とも言う。
downtown | n | 中心街 | the city center (US) | Let's go downtown. || I work at a cafe downtown. || Downtown gets dead after midnight. | travel | go downtown のように to を付けない。日本の「下町」ではなく中心街。
neighborhood | n | 近所・地域 | an area of a city | It's a safe neighborhood. || This neighborhood has the best ramen in Osaka. || The whole neighborhood can hear my drums. | travel | in my neighborhood（うちの近所で）でよく使う。イギリスつづりは neighbourhood。
block | n | 区画・ブロック | the distance between streets | It's two blocks away. || I live a few blocks from the station. || Walk two blocks and turn right. | travel | アメリカの道案内で「〜区画先」。日本の街なら「歩いて〜分」の方が伝わることも。
subway | n | 地下鉄（米） | underground train | Take the subway. || The subway is packed at rush hour. || I take the last subway home after my shift. | travel | アメリカは subway、ロンドンは the Tube。イギリスで subway は「地下道」。
ride | n | 乗せること・乗り物 | a trip in a vehicle | Do you need a ride? || Thanks for the ride! || Can I get a ride to the venue? | travel | give someone a ride で「車で送る」。乗せてほしいときは Can I get a ride?
hostel | n | ホステル | cheap shared accommodation | I stayed in a hostel. || The hostel had a rooftop bar. || Hostels are great for meeting people. | travel | 若者向けの安宿。hotel と混ざらないよう「ハーストゥル」を意識して発音。
check-in | n | チェックイン | arrival process at a hotel | Check-in is at 3 p.m. || Can we do an early check-in? || Online check-in opens 24 hours before the flight. | travel | 名詞は check-in、動詞は check in（2語）。check in on someone は「様子を見る」。
apartment | n | アパート・マンション | a home in a building | I'm looking for an apartment. || My apartment is tiny, but the rent is cheap. || I record vocals in my apartment. Sorry, neighbors. | paperwork | 日本の「マンション」も apartment。英語の mansion は大豪邸。
landlord | n | 大家 | the owner who rents a home | My landlord raised the rent. || I texted my landlord about the broken heater. || My landlord said no pets and no drums. | paperwork | 男女どちらにも landlord を使うのが今は普通。
tenant | n | 入居者 | someone who rents a home | Tenants have rights too. || The last tenant left the place a mess. || We're looking for a tenant for the second floor of the shop. | paperwork | 店舗やオフィスの入居者も tenant。「テナント料」は rent。
rent | n | 家賃 | money paid to live somewhere | Rent is due on the 1st. || Rent in Osaka is cheaper than in Tokyo. || We rent a studio by the hour. | paperwork | 動詞の rent は「借りる」。貸す側は rent out。
utilities | n | 光熱費 | electricity, water, gas | Are utilities included? || Utilities are about ten thousand yen a month. || The rent's cheap, but utilities aren't included. | paperwork | 電気・ガス・水道をまとめて utilities。ふつう複数形。
furnished | adj | 家具付きの | with furniture | Is it furnished? || The apartment comes fully furnished. || I want a furnished place so I don't have to buy anything. | paperwork | fully furnished / unfurnished でよく使う。家具は furniture（数えない名詞）。
roommate wanted | phr | ルームメイト募集 | looking for someone to share | I saw a "roommate wanted" post. || I posted a roommate wanted ad on the community board. | paperwork | 張り紙や広告の決まり文句。〜 wanted で「〜募集」（Staff wanted など）。
form | n | 書類・用紙 | a document to fill in | Please fill out this form. || I filled out the form wrong. Twice. || The form is online now. | paperwork | fill out a form（米）／ fill in a form（英）。write a form とは言わない。
signature | n | 署名 | your written name | We need your signature here. || Can I get your signature for the delivery? || That spicy ramen is the shop's signature dish. | paperwork | 有名人のサインは autograph。signature dish（看板料理）も便利。
ID | n | 身分証 | identification | Can I see your ID? || Bring a photo ID to the venue. || The bouncer checked my ID twice. | paperwork | 発音は「アイディー」。photo ID（顔写真付き身分証）と言うことが多い。
address | n | 住所 | where you live | What's your address? || Send it to my work address. || Can I get your email address? | paperwork | email address など住所以外にも使う。動詞の address は「（問題に）対処する」。
bank account | n | 銀行口座 | money stored at a bank | I opened a bank account. || Just transfer it to my bank account. || My bank account is crying after this trip. | money | open / close a bank account。口座番号は account number。
paperwork | n | 書類仕事 | official documents | The paperwork took all day. || Running a small business is fifty percent paperwork. || I'll do the paperwork after my shift. | paperwork | 数えない名詞。do the paperwork で「書類仕事をする」。
application | n | 申し込み・アプリ | a formal request / app | My application was approved. || I sent in my application for the cafe job. || Have you finished the visa application? | paperwork | スマホのアプリは app が普通。application は「申し込み」の意味が中心。
approved | adj | 承認された | officially accepted | Your visa is approved. || My loan got approved! || The design is approved. Let's print it. | paperwork | get approved の形が会話で多い。approve of ~ は「〜を良しとする」。
deadline extension | n | 締め切りの延長 | more time to finish | Can I get a deadline extension? || The client gave us a deadline extension, thank God. | paperwork | ask for an extension のように extension だけでも通じる。
emergency | n | 緊急事態 | a dangerous situation | Call 911 in an emergency. || Sorry to text so late. It's kind of an emergency. || In Japan, the emergency number is 119. | paperwork | 日本は救急・消防が119、警察が110。emergency room（ER）は救急外来。
