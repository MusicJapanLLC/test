back out | v | 手を引く・約束をやめる | decide not to do something you agreed to | You can't back out now, the flyers are already printed. || The investor backed out at the last minute. | business, friends | back out of ＋約束・契約。約束した後に「やっぱやめる」こと。cancel より責める響き。
back off | v | 引き下がる・距離を置く | stop pressuring someone | Back off, man, I'm handling it. || I backed off when I realized she wasn't interested. | conflict, crush | 命令形 Back off! は「下がれ・ほっとけ」とかなりきつい。自分に使うと「引く」。
brush off | v | 軽くあしらう・受け流す | ignore or dismiss something | He just brushed off my idea in the meeting. || I tried to apologize, but she brushed me off. | conflict, work | ホコリを払うように相手を「さらっと流す」。brush it off は「気にしないでおく」。
chip away at | v | 少しずつ減らす・削る | slowly reduce something bit by bit | I'm chipping away at my student loans. || Every gym session chips away at the stress. | money, gym | chip in（お金を出し合う）とは別物。地道に少しずつ削っていくイメージ。
come around | v | 考えを変えて受け入れる | change your mind and agree | My dad hated my tattoos, but he'll come around. || Give her time, she'll come around. | feelings, friends | 反対していた人が最終的に受け入れること。come round はイギリス式。
come down with | v | （病気に）かかる | start to get sick | I always come down with a cold after a tour. || I think I'm coming down with something. | health | come down with ＋病名。I'm coming down with something.＝なんか風邪っぽい、の定番。
come off as | v | 〜という印象を与える | seem a certain way to others | I don't want to come off as desperate. || Your text kind of came off as rude. | dm, crush | come across as とほぼ同じだが、come off as は悪い印象のときによく使う。
come through | v | 期待に応える・やってくれる | do what people needed you to do | I knew you'd come through for me. || The client finally came through with the payment. | friends, business | You came through! は「さすが！助かった！」の褒め言葉。come through for ＋人。
fall through | v | （計画が）ダメになる | fail to happen | If the venue falls through, we'll do it at the cafe. || The deal fell through at the last minute. | business, music | 計画・契約・約束が「お流れになる」。fall apart（崩壊する）より、実現しなかった感じ。
fall behind | v | 遅れをとる | fail to keep up | If I miss a week, I fall behind on everything. || We're falling behind on orders. | work, study | fall behind on ＋仕事・支払い。catch up（追いつく）の反対。
fall back on | v | （いざというとき）〜を頼りにする | use something as a backup | It's good to have a skill to fall back on. || If music doesn't work out, I can fall back on barista work. | work, money | 最後の頼み・保険。something to fall back on の形で頻出。
fall out | v | 仲たがいする | stop being friends after an argument | Don't let a few bucks make you fall out with your best friend. || We used to be close, but we fell out over money. | friends, conflict | fall out with ＋人。break up は恋人、fall out は友達・仲間とのケンカ別れ。名詞 falling-out も。
factor in | v | 考慮に入れる・計算に入れる | include something when you plan or calculate | Don't forget to factor in shipping. || Did you factor in the tax? | money, business | 見積もりや計画で「〜も込みで考える」。take into account の口語版。
hold off | v | 先延ばしにする・様子を見る | delay doing something | Let's hold off on the announcement until Monday. || I'm holding off on buying a new laptop. | business, shopping | hold off on ＋ ing / 名詞。postpone より「様子見で待つ」ニュアンス。
iron out | v | （問題を）解決する・詰める | fix small problems | We still need to iron out the details. || Let's iron out the schedule before we announce anything. | business, work | アイロンでしわを伸ばすイメージ。iron out the details / the kinks の形で。
lash out | v | （急に）キレる・食ってかかる | suddenly attack someone with words | I'm sorry I lashed out at you yesterday. || When he's stressed, he lashes out at whoever's closest. | conflict, feelings | 八つ当たり気味に、突然怒りをぶつけること。lash out at ＋人。
opt out | v | 参加しない・脱退する | choose not to take part | You can opt out of the emails anytime. || I opted out of the group trip this year. | online, paperwork | opt out of ＋物事。反対は opt in。メルマガの配信停止でもよく見る。
pan out | v | うまくいく・結果的にそうなる | turn out well | If this job doesn't pan out, I'll move back to Osaka. || Let's see how things pan out. | work, daily | 否定・疑問文で使うことが多い。work out とほぼ同じ。砂金をすくう pan から。
play down | v | 大したことないように言う | make something seem less important | The company tried to play down the data leak. || Don't play down your own work. | business, feelings | downplay と同じ意味。反対は play up（強調する）。謙遜しすぎる人への一言にも。
rope in | v | （人を）引っぱり込む | persuade someone to help or join | My sister roped me into helping at the bake sale. || Can we rope in a few more people for the shoot? | friends, work | 投げ縄で引き寄せるイメージ。半ば強引に手伝わせること。rope someone into ＋ ing。
scale back | v | 縮小する | make something smaller or less | We had to scale back the tour because of costs. || The city scaled back the fireworks this year. | business, music | 計画的に規模を小さくすること。反対は scale up。
size up | v | 品定めする・見極める | look at someone and judge them | Every new guy at the gym tries to size me up. || I could feel the other bands sizing us up backstage. | gym, business | 相手の実力・状況を「値踏みする」。size up the situation のように状況にも使う。
talk down to | v | 見下した話し方をする | speak to someone as if they're stupid | Don't talk down to me, I know how the espresso machine works. || I hate how some managers talk down to new staff. | conflict, work | look down on（見下す）の「話し方」版。子ども扱いする口調のこと。
weigh in | v | 意見を言う・議論に加わる | give your opinion in a discussion | Can I weigh in on this? || Everyone online had to weigh in on the breakup. | work, online | 議論に「参戦する」。ボクシングの計量 weigh-in から。weigh in on ＋話題。
wind down | v | 落ち着く・徐々に終わる | relax, or slowly come to an end | I need an hour to wind down after a shift. || The party started to wind down around two. | daily, bar | wind は「ワインド」と読む（風の wind ではない）。寝る前のリラックスにも、イベントの終わりにも。
zero in on | v | 〜に狙いを定める・絞り込む | focus closely on one thing | Let's zero in on the main problem. || She zeroed in on the weak part of the song right away. | work, music | 照準をゼロに合わせるイメージ。focus on の強い版。
act up | v | 調子が悪い・行儀が悪い | not work properly, or behave badly | My laptop's acting up again. || The kids always act up when they're tired. | phone, daily | 機械・体の不調にも、子どもの悪ふざけにも使う。My knee is acting up.＝膝がまた痛む。

blurt out | v | うっかり口走る | say something suddenly without thinking | I didn't mean to blurt it out. || She blurted out the surprise before the cake even came. | friends, feelings | 秘密や本音をつい言ってしまうこと。考える前に口から出る感じ。
boil down to | v | 要するに〜ということになる | have something as the main point | It all boils down to money. || What it boils down to is trust. | business, daily | 煮詰めて残るもの＝本質。It boils down to... で議論をまとめられる。
bounce back | v | 立ち直る・回復する | recover quickly | Don't worry, you'll bounce back. || The shop bounced back after a slow winter. | feelings, business | 失恋・病気・不振からの回復。ボールが跳ね返るイメージで前向き。
bottle up | v | （感情を）溜め込む | keep feelings inside | Don't bottle up your feelings, talk to someone. || I bottled it up for years and then exploded. | feelings | 瓶に詰めて栓をするイメージ。メンタルヘルスの話でよく出る。
branch out | v | 手を広げる | start doing new things | We're branching out into catering. || It's time to branch out from lo-fi. | business, music | 木の枝が広がるイメージ。branch out into ＋新分野。
brush up on | v | （スキルを）磨き直す | practice something you used to know | I need to brush up on my English before the trip. || Brush up on your chords before the session. | study, music | 忘れかけたことの「おさらい」。study（ゼロから学ぶ）とは違う。
butt in | v | 口をはさむ・割り込む | interrupt rudely | Sorry to butt in, but I think you dropped this. || He keeps butting in when I'm talking to customers. | conflict, cafe | interrupt のカジュアル版。Sorry to butt in, but... は丁寧な割り込みにも使える。
