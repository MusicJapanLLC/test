//! Collocations | make, do, take, have, get: the pairs natives use | levelup
make a decision | phr | 決断する・決める | choose something after thinking | I need to make a decision about the studio by Friday. || Don't make big decisions at 4 a.m. after a night shift. | work, daily | do a decision ✗。take a decision はイギリス寄りで、アメリカ英語なら make 一択。
make a mistake | phr | ミスをする・間違える | do something wrong by accident | Everybody makes mistakes in their first week. || I made a mistake on the invoice and charged him twice. | work, daily | do a mistake ✗ は日本人の定番ミス。ミスは make、仕事や宿題は do。
make progress | phr | 前進する・上達する | get better or move forward | You'll make progress faster if you actually sleep. || We didn't make much progress in the meeting, honestly. | gym, work | progress は数えない名詞なので a progress ✗。make real progress / make some progress の形で。
make an effort | phr | 努力する・わざわざ〜しようとする | try hard to do something | I'm trying to make an effort to call my parents more. || He didn't even make an effort to learn our names. | friends, feelings | do an effort ✗。make an effort to ＋動詞で「ちゃんと気にかけて〜する」気持ちが出る。
make a living | phr | 生計を立てる | earn enough money to live | Can you actually make a living from music these days? || She makes a living teaching yoga and selling prints online. | money, music | earn a life ✗。make a living from ＋名詞 / doing の形。「食べていく」感じ。
make a difference | phr | 違いを生む・効果がある | have a real effect | A decent mic makes a huge difference on stream. || Volunteering at church made a real difference for me. | music, faith | 「差をつける」より「良い影響がある」。It doesn't make a difference.＝どっちでも同じ。
make sense | phr | 筋が通る・理解できる | be logical or easy to understand | That doesn't make sense to me. || Does that make sense, or should I explain it again? || It makes sense to book the venue early. | work, daily | Do you understand? は上から目線に聞こえる。Does that make sense? が柔らかい確認の定番。
make a point of | phr | 必ず〜するようにしている | make sure you always do something | I make a point of thanking the kitchen staff after every shift. || She makes a point of stretching after every workout. | daily, gym | make a point of ＋ ing で「ポリシーとしてわざわざやる」。make a point（主張を通す）とは別物。
make an exception | phr | 例外にする・特別に認める | allow something you normally don't | We don't usually do refunds, but I'll make an exception this time. || Could you make an exception? I'm only five minutes late. | work, business | do an exception ✗。断るときは I can't make an exception. で丁寧に線を引ける。
make a complaint | phr | 苦情を言う | formally say you're unhappy | If the delivery's late again, I'm gonna make a complaint. || A customer made a complaint about the noise from our rehearsal. | business, shopping | 「クレームをつける」を claim と言うのは✗。claim は「主張する・請求する」で、苦情は complaint。
make a scene | phr | 人前で騒ぐ | act angry or emotional in public | Please don't make a scene in the cafe. || He made a scene when his card got declined. | conflict, cafe | 「場面を作る」ではない。人前で怒鳴ったり泣いたりして注目を集めること。
make room for | phr | 〜のために場所・余裕を空ける | create space or time for something | Can you scoot over and make room for one more? || I'm cutting a shift to make room for music. | daily, music | make a room ✗（部屋を作るになる）。room は「スペース」の意味で冠詞なし。
make time for | phr | 〜のために時間を作る | find time in a busy life | You have to make time for the people you love. || I make time for the gym even on night shift weeks. | daily, health | make a time ✗。take time（時間がかかる）と混同しやすい。make time は自分で捻出すること。
make a move | phr | 行動に出る・アプローチする | take action, often romantically | If you like her, make a move already. || It's getting late, we should make a move. | crush, friends | 恋愛の「アタックする」（make a move on someone）と、「そろそろ出発する」の2つの意味がある。
make an impression | phr | 印象を残す | affect how people see you | You only get one chance to make a first impression. || The demo made a big impression on the label. | business, work | give an impression は「〜という印象を与える」で別物。強く印象を残すなら make。
make do with | phr | 〜で間に合わせる | manage with what you have | No mic stand, so we'll make do with a stack of books. || We had to make do with instant coffee all week. | daily, money | 「ないなりに何とかする」。make do の do は原形のまま固定。
make the most of | phr | 〜を最大限に活かす | use something as well as you can | Let's make the most of the day off. || I'm making the most of the cheap rent while it lasts. | daily, travel | 良いチャンスを活かすときに使う。make the best of は「悪い状況で何とかする」。
make it up to | phr | 埋め合わせをする | do something nice after letting someone down | Sorry I missed your show. I'll make it up to you. || How can I make it up to you? Dinner's on me. | crush, friends | make up（仲直り）とは別。make it up to ＋人で「借りを返す」。ドタキャン後の定番。
make an excuse | phr | 言い訳をする | give a reason to avoid blame | Don't make excuses, just come to the gym. || He always makes some excuse to leave early. | gym, conflict | say an excuse ✗。Stop making excuses. はコーチや先輩の決まり文句。
do someone a favor | phr | 頼みを聞く | help someone as a kindness | Can you do me a favor and watch the register for a sec? || Do yourself a favor and get some sleep. | friends, work | make a favor ✗。Do yourself a favor and... は「悪いこと言わないから〜しな」。
do business with | phr | 〜と取引する | buy from or sell to someone | We'd love to do business with your cafe. || I don't do business with people who pay late. | business | make business ✗。It's a pleasure doing business with you. も商談の締めの定番。
do damage | phr | 損害を与える・ダメージを与える | cause harm | One bad review can do a lot of damage. || The typhoon did some serious damage to our sign. | business, daily | make damage ✗。damage は数えないので a damage ✗。do a lot of damage の形でよく使う。
do more harm than good | phr | 逆効果になる | cause more problems than it solves | Training through the pain does more harm than good. || Yelling at him will do more harm than good. | gym, conflict | 「逆効果」にぴったりの決まり文句。harm も数えない名詞。
do someone good | phr | 〜のためになる・気分転換になる | be good for someone | A weekend off would do you good. || Getting out of Osaka for a few days did me good. | health, feelings | make you good ✗。It'll do you good. で「気分転換になるよ」と背中を押せる。
do your homework | phr | 下調べをする | research before something important | Do your homework before you sign with a label. || She clearly did her homework on our brand. | business, music | 学校の宿題だけでなく、商談・契約・デート前の「下調べ」の意味でよく使う。
do a good job | phr | よくやる・上手くやる | do something well | Don't stress, you're doing a good job. || You did a great job on the mix. | work, music | make a good job ✗。good の代わりに great / amazing もよく使う。
do research | phr | 調べる・調査する | study a topic to find facts | Do some research before you buy a used mixer. || We're doing research on what customers in Namba actually want. | business, study | make research ✗。research は数えないので a research ✗、researches ✗。
do the math | phr | 計算してみる・考えればわかる | calculate, or think it through logically | Do the math: two shifts a week won't cover rent. || I did the math and the subscription isn't worth it. | money | 「計算すればわかるでしょ」という皮肉にもなる。make a calculation より圧倒的に口語的。
do the trick | phr | 効く・うまくいく | solve the problem | A little more reverb should do the trick. || Ginger tea usually does the trick for my throat. | music, health | 「これで解決」という軽い言い方。trick は手品ではなく「うまいやり方」。
do cardio | phr | 有酸素運動をする | do exercise that raises your heart rate | I do cardio twice a week, but I hate every minute. || Do your cardio after lifting, not before. | gym, health | play cardio ✗。運動は do（do yoga）、球技は play、〜ing 系は go（go running）。
do someone a solid | phr | 助けてあげる（スラング） | do someone a favor (slang) | Do me a solid and cover my Saturday shift? || You really did me a solid with that ride. | friends, work | do someone a favor のスラング版。友達同士専用で、上司やお客さんには使わない。
do a double take | phr | 二度見する | look again in surprise | I had to do a double take — is that our song on the radio? || She did a double take when she saw me at church. | daily, friends | 日本語の「二度見」そのもの。驚いて思わずもう一度見ること。
take a break | phr | 休憩する | stop for a short rest | Let's take a ten-minute break and come back to the chorus. || You've been grinding all week. Take a break. | work, music | rest a break ✗。have a break はイギリス寄りで、アメリカでは take が普通。
take notes | phr | メモを取る | write down important points | Take notes during the call so we don't forget the price. || Can you take notes? My hands are full. | work, study | write a memo ✗（memo は社内文書）。メモを取るは take notes。
take a risk | phr | リスクを取る・賭けに出る | do something that might fail | Sometimes you have to take a risk on a new artist. || I took a big risk quitting my job. | business, money | do a risk ✗。take risks と複数形にすると「冒険する性格」。
take responsibility | phr | 責任を取る・認める | accept that something is your duty or fault | I'll take responsibility for the mistake. || You need to take responsibility for your own health. | work, health | have responsibility は「担当している」。ミスを認めるなら take。
take advantage of | phr | 〜を活用する・〜につけこむ | use an opportunity, or use someone unfairly | Take advantage of the free trial while it lasts. || I think he's taking advantage of you. | business, conflict | 物なら「活用」、人なら「つけこむ」。人に使うと悪い意味になるので注意。
take it personally | phr | 自分への攻撃と受け取る | feel hurt as if it were about you | Don't take it personally, he's rude to everyone. || I took it personally when they cut my song from the set. | feelings, work | Don't take it personally. で「あなたのせいじゃないから気にしないで」。
take something seriously | phr | 〜を真剣に受け止める | treat something as important | You need to take your sleep seriously. || Nobody took me seriously until the numbers came in. | health, work | take serious ✗。形容詞ではなく副詞 seriously。something の位置に目的語が入る。
take a hint | phr | 察する | understand an indirect message | She kept checking her phone, but he couldn't take a hint. || Take the hint, bro. She's not interested. | crush, dm | 遠回しなサインに気づくこと。can't take a hint＝空気が読めない。
take a hit | phr | 打撃を受ける | suffer a loss or damage | Our sales take a hit every rainy season. || My confidence took a hit after that audition. | business, feelings | 売上・評判・自信が「ダメージを受ける」。get a hit ✗。
take turns | phr | 交代でやる | do something one after another | We take turns closing the shop. || Let's take turns on the bench. | gym, work | do turns ✗。turns は必ず複数形。take turns ＋ ing の形も。
take your word for it | phr | 君の言うことを信じる | believe what you say without checking | Fine, I'll take your word for it. || Don't take my word for it — try the cold brew yourself. | friends, cafe | Don't take my word for it. は「自分で試してみて」という勧め文句。
take a toll on | phr | 〜にこたえる・悪影響を及ぼす | slowly damage | Night shifts take a toll on your body. || The breakup took a toll on him. | health, feelings | ダメージがじわじわ蓄積するイメージ。toll は「代償・犠牲」。
take credit for | phr | 〜を自分の手柄にする | claim praise for something | He always takes credit for my ideas. || I can't take credit for the beat — my producer made it. | work, music | 手柄の横取りへの不満でよく出る。give someone credit は逆に「功績を認める」。
take a step back | phr | 一歩引いて考える | pause and look at things calmly | Let's take a step back and look at the numbers. || I had to take a step back from dating for a while. | work, feelings | 物理的に下がるより、「冷静になる・距離を置く」比喩でよく使う。
take for granted | phr | 当たり前だと思う | not appreciate something because you're used to it | Don't take your health for granted. || I took my mom's cooking for granted until I moved out. | feelings, faith | 感謝が足りなかったニュアンス。take it for granted that... の形も。
take medicine | phr | 薬を飲む | swallow medicine | Did you take your medicine today? || Take this medicine twice a day after meals. | health | drink medicine ✗ は超定番ミス。薬は錠剤でも液体でも take。
take sides | phr | どちらかの味方をする | support one person in an argument | I don't want to take sides, you two figure it out. || My roommate always takes my girlfriend's side. | conflict, friends | take someone's side＝〜の味方をする。I'm not taking sides. で中立宣言。
take a joke | phr | 冗談を受け流す | not get upset when teased | Relax, can't you take a joke? || He can dish it out, but he can't take a joke. | friends, conflict | 「冗談が通じない」は can't take a joke。ダークなノリの友達との定番。
take time off | phr | 休みを取る | have a break from work | I need to take some time off after the tour. || She took two weeks off to visit family in Okinawa. | work, travel | get a holiday ✗ はアメリカでは不自然。休暇は take time off / take a day off。
take it out on | phr | 八つ当たりする | treat someone badly because you're upset | Don't take it out on me, I didn't break the printer. || He takes it out on the new guy whenever the boss yells at him. | conflict, work | 「八つ当たり」にぴったり。it はイライラそのもの、on の後に被害者。

pay your dues | phr | 下積みをする | work hard for years before success | Every artist has to pay their dues. || I paid my dues washing dishes before I got to cook. | music, work | dues＝会費→下積み。成功した人が昔の苦労を語るときの定番。
break a promise | phr | 約束を破る | not do what you promised | I hate to break a promise, but I can't make it Friday. || He broke his promise again. | friends, conflict | 守る＝keep、破る＝break。make a promise（約束する）とセットで覚える。
break the news | phr | （悪い）知らせを伝える | tell someone bad or surprising news | How do I break the news to my parents? || The manager broke the news about the store closing. | work, feelings | 悪い知らせを切り出すときに多い。announce より個人的で気をつかう感じ。
run errands | phr | 用事を済ませる | do small jobs like shopping or banking | I've got to run some errands before work. || I spent the whole morning running errands. | daily | do errands より run が自然。銀行・郵便局・買い出しなどの細かい用事。
run the numbers | phr | 試算する | calculate to check something | Let me run the numbers before we decide. || I ran the numbers and we're barely breaking even. | business, money | do the math より仕事寄り。価格設定や投資判断の前に。
run a fever | phr | 熱がある | have a high temperature | He's running a fever, so he's staying home. || If you run a fever, don't come in. | health | have a fever と同じ。「熱を出している」状態を run で表す。
give it a shot | phr | やってみる | try something | I've never done stand-up, but I'll give it a shot. || Give it a shot — what's the worst that could happen? | daily, music | try it より前向きでカジュアル。give it a try / give it a go も同じ。
give someone a ride | phr | 車で送る | drive someone somewhere | Can you give me a ride to the station? || I'll give you a ride home after the gig. | travel, friends | send you ✗ では「車で送る」にならない。車なら give someone a ride / drive someone。
give someone a hard time | phr | 〜を困らせる・いじる | criticize or tease someone | My coworkers give me a hard time about my accent. || Don't give him a hard time, it's his first day. | work, friends | have a hard time（苦労する）と立場が逆。軽いいじりにも本気の叱責にも使う。
give someone space | phr | そっとしておく | let someone be alone for a while | I think you should give her some space. || Give me some space, I'll text you when I'm ready. | crush, feelings | ケンカや別れ話の後の定番。space は「距離・時間」。
meet a deadline | phr | 締め切りに間に合わせる | finish work by the due date | If you can't meet the deadline, tell me early. || We worked all night to meet the deadline. | work, business | keep a deadline ✗、catch a deadline ✗。間に合わせるは meet、遅れたら miss。
meet expectations | phr | 期待に応える | be as good as people hoped | The new menu didn't quite meet expectations. || I'm scared my second EP won't meet expectations. | business, music | answer expectations ✗（「期待に応える」の直訳ミス）。正解は meet。
throw a party | phr | パーティーを開く | host a party | We're throwing a party for the shop's first anniversary. || Let's throw him a surprise party. | friends, bar | open a party ✗。パーティーは throw か have。
lose track of time | phr | 時間を忘れる | not notice how much time has passed | I always lose track of time in the studio. || Sorry I'm late, I totally lost track of time. | music, daily | 遅刻の言い訳の定番。forget the time ✗ より自然。
lose your temper | phr | キレる・かっとなる | suddenly get very angry | I try not to lose my temper with customers. || He lost his temper over a parking spot. | conflict, feelings | get angry より「我慢の糸が切れた」感じ。
pull an all-nighter | phr | 徹夜する | stay awake all night to work | I had to pull an all-nighter to finish the mix. || Don't pull an all-nighter before the exam. | study, music | stay up all night と同じ意味。pull は「やり切る」感じ。
pull a double | phr | 2シフト連続で働く | work two shifts in a row | I'm pulling a double on Saturday. || If you pull a double, you get Monday off. | work | double shift の略。飲食・病院・工場などのシフト勤務で頻出。
hit it off | phr | すぐ意気投合する | like each other right away | We hit it off right away. || I knew you two would hit it off. | crush, friends | 初対面で気が合うこと。過去形も hit のまま。
drop the ball | phr | へまをする・やるべきことを落とす | fail to do your part | Sorry, I dropped the ball on that email. || We can't drop the ball on this order. | work | I dropped the ball. は自分のミスを認める大人の謝り方で、仕事で頻出。
go viral | phr | バズる | spread fast online | That clip could go viral. || Our latte art video went viral overnight. | online | 「バズる」に一番近い。buzz は英語ではこの意味にならない。
split the bill | phr | 割り勘にする | share the cost | Let's split the bill. || We always split the bill on first dates. | food, crush | go Dutch は古め。Let's split it. だけでも通じる。
hold a grudge | phr | 根に持つ | stay angry about something for a long time | Life's too short to hold a grudge. || Is he still holding a grudge about the gig? | conflict, faith | grudge＝恨み。I don't hold grudges. で「根に持たないタイプ」。
save face | phr | 面目を保つ | avoid embarrassment in front of others | He said he was busy just to save face. || Let him save face — don't correct him in front of everyone. | work, conflict | 日本語の「メンツを保つ」とほぼ同じ発想。逆は lose face。
strike a balance | phr | バランスを取る | find a good middle point | It's hard to strike a balance between music and a day job. || You have to strike a balance between price and quality. | work, music | take a balance ✗。会話なら find a balance も可。
tell the difference | phr | 違いがわかる・見分ける | notice how things are different | Can you tell the difference between these two mixes? || Honestly, I can't tell the difference. | music, food | know the difference は「違いを知っている」。見分ける・聞き分けるなら tell。
play hard to get | phr | 気のないふりをする・じらす | pretend not to be interested | Is she playing hard to get, or is she just not into me? || Stop playing hard to get and text him back. | crush, dm | 恋愛の駆け引き。わざと返信を遅らせるなど。
say grace | phr | 食前の祈りをする | pray before a meal | Let's say grace before we eat. || My grandma always says grace, even at restaurants. | faith, food | grace＝恵み。教会の食事会や家族の食卓で。
