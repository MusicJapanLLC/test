//! News & Business C1 | The words in podcasts, earnings calls and the news | levelup
volatility | n | 変動の大きさ・不安定さ | how fast and how much something changes | Crypto's volatility is why I only invest what I can afford to lose. || Analysts expect more volatility in the yen this quarter. | money, business | 形容詞は volatile（不安定な・キレやすい）。人の性格にも使える。
earnings | n | （企業の）利益・決算 | the profit a company reports | The company's earnings beat expectations. || Everyone's waiting for the earnings call on Thursday. | business, money | 個人の「稼ぎ」にも使うが、ニュースでは決算。earnings call＝決算説明会。beat / miss expectations とセットで。
guidance | n | （業績）見通し・指針 | a company's forecast for future results | The company cut its guidance for next year. || They raised full-year guidance after a strong quarter. | business | 決算ニュースでは「業績予想」。raise / cut guidance で株価が動く。普通の「指導」の意味もある。
projection | n | 予測・見込み | an estimate of future numbers | Our sales projections were way too optimistic. || Based on current projections, we'll break even in March. | business, money | 決算・事業計画の「予測値」。project（予測する）の名詞形。forecast より数字寄り。
shareholder | n | 株主 | someone who owns shares in a company | Shareholders weren't happy with the CEO's pay. || The shareholder meeting got pretty heated. | business, money | stockholder とほぼ同じ。stakeholder（利害関係者）と混同しやすいので注意。
valuation | n | 企業価値の評価額 | how much a company is judged to be worth | The startup hit a billion-dollar valuation. || Their valuation dropped after the scandal. | business, money | value の名詞形だが「評価額」の意味で使う。スタートアップの資金調達ニュースで頻出。
equity | n | 株式・持ち分・公平性 | ownership in a company, or fairness | We gave our first engineer some equity instead of a big salary. || The company says equity is part of its hiring policy. | business | ビジネスでは「持ち分」。sweat equity＝労働で得る持ち分。社会問題の話では「公平性」。
asset | n | 資産・強み | something valuable you own, or a strong point | Our biggest asset is our regular customers. || She's a real asset to the team. | business, money | 帳簿上の資産だけでなく、人を「戦力・強み」と褒めるときにも使う。
liability | n | 法的責任・負債・お荷物 | a legal duty, a debt, or a person who causes problems | The waiver protects the gym from liability. || Honestly, he's become a liability on stage. | business, paperwork | asset の反対。法律の「賠償責任」と、人を「お荷物」と言う口語の両方。
fine print | n | 細かい注意書き・小さな文字の条件 | the small details in a contract that are easy to miss | Always read the fine print before you sign. || The free trial sounded great until I read the fine print. | paperwork, shopping | 契約書やキャンペーンの「小さく書かれた不利な条件」。read the fine print で決まり文句。
merger | n | 合併 | two companies joining into one | The merger will create the biggest drugstore chain in Kansai. || Hundreds of jobs could be cut after the merger. | business | merge（合併する・合流する）の名詞形。M&A の M。
acquisition | n | 買収・獲得 | buying a company, or gaining something | The acquisition was announced this morning. || Customer acquisition is getting more expensive every year. | business | M&A の A。マーケティングでは customer acquisition＝新規顧客獲得。
IPO | abbr | 新規株式公開・上場 | when a company first sells shares to the public | The app is planning an IPO next year. || Their IPO made the early employees rich. | business, money | initial public offering の略。go public（上場する）も同じ意味で頻出。
bankruptcy | n | 破産・倒産 | being legally unable to pay your debts | The chain filed for bankruptcy last month. || One bad year almost pushed us into bankruptcy. | business, money | file for bankruptcy＝破産申請する。形容詞を使うなら go bankrupt（倒産する）。
restructuring | n | 事業再編 | a big change to how a company is organized | The restructuring will close twenty stores. || After the restructuring, my whole team had a new boss. | business, work | 日本語の「リストラ」は解雇の意味だが、英語の restructuring は組織再編全般。解雇は layoffs。
layoff | n | 人員削減・一時解雇 | job cuts made by a company | The company announced layoffs on Monday. || Half my friends in tech got hit by layoffs. | work, business | 動詞は lay off（2語）。名詞は1語で、複数形 layoffs でよく使う。日本語のリストラはこれ。
severance | n | 解雇手当・退職金 | money paid when your job is cut | They offered me three months' severance. || Read the severance agreement before you sign anything. | work, money | severance pay とも。定年の退職金（retirement allowance）とは別物。
turnout | n | 参加者数・投票率 | the number of people who come to an event or vote | Great turnout tonight, thanks for coming out! || Voter turnout was the lowest in years. | music, faith | ライブ・教会イベントの「集まり具合」にも選挙の「投票率」にも。turnover（離職）と混同注意。
churn | n | 解約率・顧客離れ | the rate at which customers stop paying | Our churn went up after the price change. || Low churn means people actually like the product. | business | サブスク業界の必須用語。churn rate とも。動詞では「解約していく」。
retention | n | 維持・定着率 | keeping customers or staff | Customer retention is cheaper than finding new people. || The bonus is meant to improve retention. | business, work | churn の反対の発想。retain（保つ）の名詞形。
market share | n | 市場シェア | a company's part of all sales in a market | They're losing market share to cheaper brands. || We're small, but our market share in Osaka is growing. | business | share だけだと「株」の意味にもなるので market share と言い切る。gain / lose market share。
bubble | n | バブル・閉じた世界 | prices far above real value, or a closed group | Some people think AI stocks are a bubble. || Living in your own online bubble makes everything look like a crisis. | money, online | 経済のバブル崩壊（the bubble bursts）と、同じ意見ばかりの「狭い世界」の2つ。
speculation | n | 憶測・投機 | guessing without facts, or risky investing | There's a lot of speculation about who's buying the company. || The price jump was mostly speculation. | business, money | ニュースでは「憶測」。動詞は speculate。金融では「投機」。
watchdog | n | 監視機関・お目付け役 | an organization that checks companies or the government | The consumer watchdog is looking into the hidden fees. || A privacy watchdog fined the app last year. | business, online | 番犬から。ニュースで規制当局や監視団体を指す短い言い方。
tailwinds | n | 追い風・有利な状況 | conditions that help progress | The weak yen is a huge tailwind for tourism. || We had some tailwinds this year, so let's not get cocky. | business, travel | 決算説明会の定番で、反対は headwinds（逆風）。単数でも複数でも使う。
headwinds | n | 逆風・不利な状況 | problems that slow progress | The company is facing serious headwinds this year. || Higher rice prices are real headwinds for restaurants. | business | 決算説明会の定番。反対は tailwinds（追い風）。複数形でよく使う。
fallout | n | 余波・悪影響 | the bad results that follow an event | The fallout from the scandal cost them half their sponsors. || I'm still dealing with the fallout from that fight. | business, conflict | 元は放射性降下物。事件や騒動の後に続く「余波」。
markup | n | 上乗せ額・値入れ | the amount added to cost to set a price | The markup on coffee is huge. || What kind of markup do you put on merch? | business, money | 原価にいくら乗せるか。margin（利益率）とは計算の基準が違う。
inventory | n | 在庫・棚卸し | the goods a business has in stock | We're running low on inventory before the holidays. || I do inventory every Sunday night after close. | business, shopping | stock とほぼ同じ。do / take inventory＝棚卸しする。
foot traffic | n | 来店客数・人通り | the number of people walking into or past a shop | Foot traffic dropped when the station exit moved. || We get way more foot traffic on weekends. | business, shopping | 小売・飲食で「人通り・来店数」。web traffic（アクセス数）との対比で使う。
cash-strapped | adj | 資金不足の・金欠の | not having enough money | A lot of cash-strapped students skip breakfast. || The city is too cash-strapped to fix the park. | money, business | ニュースの定番形容詞。口語なら broke。strap＝締めつけられているイメージ。
lucrative | adj | もうかる | making a lot of money | Wedding gigs are surprisingly lucrative. || It's a lucrative market, but it's crowded. | business, money | profitable より「うまみがある」感じ。発音は LOO-kruh-tiv。
viable | adj | 実現可能な・商売として成り立つ | able to work or succeed | Is a vinyl-only label even viable anymore? || We need a viable plan before we ask the bank for money. | business | 「ちゃんと回るか」の判断に使う。commercially viable の形でよく出る。
clickbait | n | 釣り見出し・クリックベイト | headlines made to get clicks, not to inform | That headline is pure clickbait. || I don't do clickbait thumbnails, even if they work. | online | 「釣りタイトル」のこと。形容詞的に clickbait title とも。
saturated | adj | 飽和状態の | so full there's no room for more | The coffee market in Osaka is pretty saturated. || The streaming scene is saturated with copycats. | business | 競合が多すぎて入る余地がない状態。oversaturated で強調。
spin | n | 都合のいい言い方・印象操作 | a way of presenting facts to look good | The company put a positive spin on the layoffs. || Don't believe the spin, look at the numbers. | business, online | PR の「印象操作」。put a spin on ＋物事。spin doctor＝広報の達人（皮肉）。
consolidate | v | 統合する・固める | combine things into one, or make something stronger | We consolidated three suppliers into one to save money. || Let's consolidate our lead before we expand. | business, money | 借金の一本化（consolidate debt）、地位を固める、の2つでよく使う。
paywall | n | 有料の壁・有料記事 | a system that makes you pay to read content | The best articles are always behind a paywall. || We're putting our tutorials behind a paywall. | online, business | ニュースサイトや配信の「ここから先は有料」。behind a paywall の形で。
venture capital | n | ベンチャーキャピタル | money invested in young, risky companies | They raised venture capital to open five more stores. || A lot of venture capital is going into AI right now. | business, money | 略して VC。VC は投資会社そのものも指す。
burn rate | n | 資金燃焼率 | how fast a company spends its money | Our burn rate is too high for a three-person team. || Cut the burn rate or we're out of cash by summer. | business, money | スタートアップが毎月出ていくお金。runway（残り期間）とセットで使う。
runway | n | 資金が尽きるまでの期間 | how long a company can last before money runs out | We have about eight months of runway. || The new funding gives us a lot more runway. | business | 滑走路から。飛び立つ（黒字化する）までに使える残り期間。
due diligence | n | 事前調査・精査 | careful research before a deal | Do your due diligence before you sign the lease. || The investors are still doing due diligence. | business, paperwork | 投資・買収・契約前の徹底調査。口語なら do your homework。
damage control | n | 事態の収拾・火消し | actions to limit harm after a mistake | The brand is in full damage control after the leak. || I sent ten apology texts. Total damage control. | business, online | 炎上・失言の後の「火消し」。日常の自虐にも使える。
surge | v | 急増する・急騰する | rise suddenly and strongly | Ticket sales surged after the video went viral. || Delivery orders surge every time it rains. | business, money | 名詞も同形（a surge in demand）。soar より「どっと押し寄せる」感じ。
plummet | v | 急落する | fall very fast | Sales plummeted after the bad reviews. || The yen plummeted overnight. | money, business | drop より劇的で、ニュース見出しの定番。反対は soar。
soar | v | 急上昇する | rise quickly to a high level | Rents have soared in Osaka since the tourists came back. || Our streams soared after the playlist feature. | money, music | 空に舞い上がるイメージ。soaring prices（高騰）の形もよく出る。
rebound | v | 持ち直す・反発する | go back up after a fall | Tourism has rebounded since the pandemic. || The market rebounded after Friday's drop. | business, travel | 景気・株価に使う。恋愛の rebound（失恋直後の反動の恋）は名詞で別の意味。
bullish | adj | 強気の・楽観的な | confident that prices or things will go up | Investors are bullish on Japanese stocks. || I'm pretty bullish on this new single. | money, music | 反対は bearish（弱気の）。相場だけでなく「〜に期待している」の口語にも。
spike | n | 急増・急上昇 | a sudden sharp increase | We saw a spike in orders after the post. || There's been a spike in scam calls lately. | business, phone | グラフの尖った山。動詞でも Prices spiked. と使える。surge より短期的。
dip | n | 一時的な下落 | a small short drop | There's always a dip in sales after New Year's. || Buy the dip, they said. It kept dipping. | business, money | 大きく落ちるなら plummet、ちょっと下がるなら dip。buy the dip＝押し目買い。
downturn | n | 景気後退・低迷 | a period when business gets worse | Restaurants got hit hard in the downturn. || The music industry survived worse downturns than this. | business, money | recession（不況）より軽く、業界レベルでも使える。反対は upturn。
slump | n | 不振・スランプ | a period of poor results | The whole industry is in a slump. || I'm in a creative slump and nothing sounds good. | business, music | 売上・景気の「不振」と、創作やスポーツの「スランプ」の両方。
sluggish | adj | 鈍い・伸びない | slow and lacking energy | Sales have been sluggish all spring. || I feel sluggish if I skip my morning coffee. | business, health | 景気にも体調にも使える。slow より「だるそう」なニュアンス。
robust | adj | 堅調な・頑丈な | strong and healthy | Demand for vintage gear is still robust. || We need a more robust system for online orders. | business | 経済ニュースで「堅調」。システムの話なら「堅牢な」。
stagnant | adj | 停滞した・よどんだ | not growing, moving or changing | Wages have been stagnant for decades. || I felt stagnant at my old job, so I quit. | money, work | 経済・給料にも、自分のキャリアの「停滞感」にも。sluggish より完全に止まっている感じ。
obsolete | adj | 時代遅れの・廃れた | no longer used | My old audio interface is basically obsolete. || Some people think AI will make certain jobs obsolete. | business, music | outdated より強く「もう使い物にならない」。make ＋物 ＋ obsolete の形も。
emerging | adj | 新興の・台頭しつつある | starting to grow or become known | She's one of the most exciting emerging artists in Kansai. || Emerging markets are risky but fast-growing. | music, business | new より「伸びてきている」感じ。emerging markets＝新興国市場。
unprecedented | adj | 前例のない | never happened before | The demand for tickets was unprecedented. || We're living in unprecedented times, apparently. | business, daily | ニュースで使われすぎて、少し皮肉に聞こえることも。precedent＝前例。
boycott | n | 不買運動・ボイコット | refusing to buy or use something as a protest | People started a boycott after the CEO's comments. || The boycott barely hurt their sales, honestly. | shopping, online | 動詞も同形（boycott a brand）。call for a boycott＝不買を呼びかける。
dividend | n | 配当・見返り | money a company pays to shareholders | The company raised its dividend this year. || All that practice is finally paying dividends. | money, business | pay dividends は「（努力が）後で実を結ぶ」の比喩としても頻出。
turmoil | n | 混乱・大荒れ | a state of confusion and trouble | The company has been in turmoil since the founder left. || Markets were in turmoil after the announcement. | business, feelings | 政治・市場・組織の大混乱。心の動揺（emotional turmoil）にも使う。
subsidy | n | 補助金 | money the government gives to support something | The city offers a subsidy for small shop renovations. || Without the subsidy, the farm would close. | business, money | 政府・自治体からの補助金。grant は個別に申請してもらう助成金のイメージ。
tariff | n | 関税 | a tax on imported goods | New tariffs could raise the price of guitars. || The tariff fight is hurting small exporters. | business, money | ニュース頻出。impose tariffs（関税を課す）の形で。
stimulus | n | 景気刺激策・刺激 | government spending to boost the economy | The government announced a new stimulus package. || The stimulus checks helped a lot of people pay rent. | money, business | stimulus package / stimulus check がニュースの定番。複数形は stimuli。
deficit | n | 赤字・不足 | spending more than you earn | The government is running a huge deficit. || I'm in a calorie deficit to cut weight. | money, gym | 国の財政赤字にも、ダイエットの calorie deficit にも使える。反対は surplus。
GDP | abbr | 国内総生産 | the total value of everything a country produces | Japan's GDP grew slightly last quarter. || Tourism is a big part of the local GDP. | business, money | gross domestic product の略。G-D-P と一文字ずつ読む。
shortage | n | 不足 | not enough of something | There's a labor shortage at every restaurant in town. || The egg shortage made our brunch menu impossible. | business, food | labor shortage（人手不足）は日本のニュースでも定番。lack より「供給が足りない」感じ。
supply chain | n | サプライチェーン・供給網 | the steps that bring a product to the customer | Supply chain problems delayed our merch by a month. || One port strike can break the whole supply chain. | business | 原料から店頭までの流れ全体。supply chain issues はコロナ以降の頻出語。
disruption | n | 混乱・破壊的変化 | a break in normal activity, or a big change to an industry | The strike caused major disruption on the trains. || Streaming was the biggest disruption the music business ever saw. | business, travel | 交通の「乱れ」と、業界を変える「破壊的イノベーション」の2つ。
cost of living | n | 生活費 | how much money you need for basic things | The cost of living in Tokyo is way higher than in Osaka. || Wages aren't keeping up with the cost of living. | money, daily | cost-of-living crisis（生活費危機）もニュースの定番。
wage | n | 賃金・時給 | money paid for work, usually by the hour | The cafe raised its starting wage. || Minimum wage still isn't enough to live on. | work, money | salary は月給・年俸、wage は時給・日給。minimum wage＝最低賃金。
strike | n | ストライキ | a protest where workers stop working | The train strike left thousands stuck. || The writers went on strike for better pay. | work, travel | go on strike＝ストに入る。野球のストライクとは別の意味でニュース頻出。
exchange rate | n | 為替レート | the value of one currency against another | The exchange rate makes Japan super cheap for tourists right now. || Check the exchange rate before you pay in dollars. | money, travel | 円安は a weak yen、円高は a strong yen。rate だけだと金利と紛らわしい。
regulation | n | 規制・規則 | an official rule | New regulations on food labels start next year. || Honestly, the industry needs more regulation. | business, paperwork | 可算なら個々の規則、不可算なら「規制」全般。動詞は regulate。
compliance | n | 法令遵守・準拠 | following rules or laws | Our compliance team checks every ad before it goes live. || Is the new menu in compliance with the allergy rules? | business, paperwork | 日本語の「コンプライアンス」と同じだが、in compliance with ＋規則 の形でよく使う。
transparency | n | 透明性 | being open and honest about information | Customers want transparency about where the beans come from. || The company promised more transparency after the leak. | business | transparent（透明な・見え透いた）の名詞形。
fraud | n | 詐欺・不正 | illegal deception for money | The founder was charged with fraud. || That online store is a total fraud. | business, money | scam より法律寄り・規模が大きい。人を指して He's a fraud.＝偽物・ペテン師。
oversight | n | 監督・見落とし | supervision, or a mistake from not noticing | The project failed because there was no oversight. || Sorry, that was an oversight on my part. | work, business | 「監督」と「見落とし」の正反対の2つの意味がある。謝罪では後者。
scrutiny | n | 厳しい監視・精査 | close and critical examination | The app is under scrutiny for how it uses data. || Famous people live under constant scrutiny. | business, online | under scrutiny の形が定番。check より厳しく、疑いの目で見る感じ。
probe | n | 調査・捜査 | an official investigation | Police launched a probe into the fire. || The probe found no evidence of fraud. | business, paperwork | ニュース見出しで investigation の代わりに使われる短い語。動詞も同形。
allegation | n | 申し立て・疑惑 | a claim that someone did something wrong, without proof | He denies all the allegations. || The allegations came out on social media first. | conflict, online | まだ証明されていない「疑惑」。allegedly（〜とされる）とセットで覚える。
whistleblower | n | 内部告発者 | someone who reports wrongdoing inside an organization | A whistleblower leaked the safety reports. || The company was accused of punishing the whistleblower. | business, work | blow the whistle（笛を吹く→告発する）から。
lawsuit | n | 訴訟 | a legal case against someone | The label is facing a lawsuit over unpaid royalties. || If you use that sample, you're asking for a lawsuit. | business, music | file a lawsuit＝訴える。動詞 sue とセットで覚える。
verdict | n | 評決・判定 | a final decision, in court or in general | The jury reached a verdict after two days. || So what's the verdict — do you like the new menu? | business, food | 裁判の評決だけでなく、So what's the verdict? で「で、感想は？」と軽く聞ける。
crackdown | n | 取り締まり | strong official action against something | There's a crackdown on illegal parking near the station. || The crackdown on fake reviews hit a lot of shops. | business, daily | 名詞は1語。動詞は crack down on（取り締まる）。
red tape | n | お役所仕事・煩雑な手続き | too many official rules and forms | The red tape to open a bar in Japan is insane. || We lost three months to red tape. | paperwork, business | 昔の公文書を赤いひもで縛っていたことから。bureaucracy の口語版。
pundit | n | 評論家・コメンテーター | an expert who gives opinions in the media | Political pundits got the election completely wrong. || Every pundit on that podcast thinks they know everything. | online, business | テレビやポッドキャストの「識者」。少し皮肉っぽく使われることが多い。
outage | n | 停電・システム障害 | a time when power or a service stops working | A power outage killed the whole livestream. || The payment app had an outage during the lunch rush. | phone, online | power outage＝停電、service outage＝サービス障害。
breach | n | 違反・侵害・流出 | breaking a rule, or a break-in to data | The data breach exposed millions of emails. || Missing the delivery date is a breach of contract. | business, online | data breach（情報漏えい）と breach of contract（契約違反）が2大定番。
recall | n | リコール・製品回収 | when a company asks buyers to return a faulty product | The company issued a recall on the frying pans. || Check if your car's part of the recall. | shopping, business | 動詞では「思い出す」の意味もあるが、ニュースでは製品回収。
misinformation | n | 誤情報・デマ | false information that spreads | Misinformation spreads faster than the truth. || Don't share that, it's misinformation. | online | 意図なしの誤情報。わざと流すデマは disinformation と区別される。
inequality | n | 不平等・格差 | an unfair difference between groups | Income inequality keeps getting worse. || The documentary is about inequality in the music industry. | money, business | equality（平等）の反対。income / wealth inequality＝所得・資産格差。
