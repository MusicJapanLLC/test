// Slang & internet English (US, 2020s). Explicit words are marked in the Japanese gloss.
vibe | n | 雰囲気・ノリ | the feeling of a place or person | This café has a good vibe. || I'm not feeling the vibe here. Let's go. || The new track has a late-night vibe. | friends | good vibe / chill vibe のように形容詞をつけて使う。動詞 vibe with ~ は「〜と気が合う」。

lowkey | adv | ちょっと・密かに | a little, secretly | I'm lowkey nervous. || I lowkey want to quit and open a bar. || Lowkey, the cheap coffee tastes better. | friends | 「ちょっと・密かに」。文頭で Lowkey, ~ と前置きにも使う。
highkey | adv | めっちゃ・堂々と | very, openly | I highkey love this song. || I highkey need a nap. || She highkey hates her new job. | friends | lowkey の反対で「めっちゃ・隠さず」。lowkey ほど頻繁には使われない。

cap | n | 嘘 | a lie | That's cap. You didn't run 20K. || He says he sells out every show. Cap. || Stop the cap. You were asleep at nine. | friends | That's cap. で「嘘でしょ」。帽子の絵文字で表すこともある。

sus | adj | 怪しい | suspicious | That link looks sus. || Kinda sus that he never shows his face on stream. || The guy asking for my bank info was super sus. | online | suspicious の略。ゲーム発祥のネットスラング。

main character | n | 主人公（気取り） | someone acting like the star | He walks in like he's the main character. || Walking home in the rain with headphones on is main character energy. || Don't be the main character at someone else's wedding. | online | main character energy（主人公オーラ）がよく使われる。良くも悪くも言う。
