// Core verbs you can't avoid (and that listening tests love)
accept | v | 受け入れる | say yes to / agree to take | I accept the offer. || We don't accept cards. Cash only. || You have to accept that some people won't like your music. | business | accept an offer / accept cards が定番。except（〜以外）と発音が似ているので注意。
achieve | v | 達成する | succeed in doing | You can achieve a lot in a year. || I finally achieved my goal of a hundred-kilo bench. || What do you want to achieve this year? | study | achieve a goal / achieve success が定番。do や get より達成感が強い。
adjust | v | 調整する・慣れる | change a little / get used to | I'm still adjusting to the night shift. || Can you adjust the mic a little lower? || It took me months to adjust to life in Osaka. | work | adjust to ~（〜に慣れる）の to の後ろは名詞か -ing。
agree | v | 賛成する | have the same opinion | I totally agree. || We agreed to split the rent. || I agree with you on the chorus, but not the bridge. | daily | agree with 人、agree on 内容、agree to 提案。I'm agree. は間違い。
allow | v | 許す・許可する | let someone do | Dogs aren't allowed inside. || My landlord doesn't allow pets. || Allow me to introduce our new barista. | daily | allow 人 to do の形。be allowed to ~（〜してよい）は受け身でよく使う。
announce | v | 発表する | tell people officially | They announced the winner. || We're announcing the tour dates tomorrow. || The pastor announced the new service times. | business | 公式に発表する感じ。会話の「みんなに言う」なら tell everyone で十分。
arrange | v | 手配する・並べる | plan or organize | I'll arrange a meeting. || Can you arrange the chairs in a circle? || I arranged the song for piano and strings. | work | 音楽では「編曲する」。arrange for ~ で「手配する」。
arrive | v | 到着する | reach a place | When does your flight arrive? || We arrived at the venue two hours early. || Your package arrived while you were at work. | travel | arrive at（建物・地点）、arrive in（都市・国）。arrive to は間違い。
attend | v | 出席する | go to an event | Will you attend the meeting? || Over two hundred people attended the show. || I attend a small church in Osaka. | work | attend the meeting（to は不要）。attend to ~ は「対応する」で別の意味。
behave | v | 振る舞う | act in a certain way | Behave yourself. || The crowd behaved really well tonight. || My laptop is behaving weird today. | friends | Behave yourself. は子どもへの「お行儀よくね」。機械の調子にも使う。
believe | v | 信じる | think something is true | I can't believe it's Friday already. || I believe in you. || Believe it or not, I used to be shy. | daily | believe 人 は「言葉を信じる」、believe in 人 は「人柄や力を信じる」。
belong | v | 属する・〜のものである | be owned by / fit in a place | Does this belong to you? || I finally feel like I belong here. || Your coffee cup belongs in the sink, not on my desk. | daily | belong to ~（〜のもの）。居場所感は I belong here. のように to なしでも言う。
breathe | v | 呼吸する | take air in and out | Just breathe. You got this. || Breathe in for four, out for four. || I can't breathe in this crowd. | health | 動詞 breathe（ブリーズ）と名詞 breath（ブレス）を混同しやすい。
chase | v | 追いかける | run after | Stop chasing people who don't text back. || I'm chasing a sound I heard in a dream. || The kids chased the pigeons around the park. | crush | chase a dream（夢を追う）。chase the bus のように物理的にも使う。
claim | v | 主張する・請求する | say something is true / ask for | He claims he's never late. || You can claim the cost of the mic on your taxes. || Where do I claim my luggage? | conflict | 日本語の「クレーム（苦情）」は complaint。claim は「主張する・請求する」。
collect | v | 集める | gather together | I collect old vinyl records. || We collect donations at church every month. || Can you collect the empty cups? | music | 回収する・趣味で集める。人や情報を集めるのは gather が多い。
compare | v | 比べる | look at differences | Don't compare yourself to others. || Compare prices before you buy a new laptop. || Compared to Tokyo, Osaka is way more relaxed. | feelings | compare A with / to B。compared to ~（〜と比べると）は会話で頻出。
compete | v | 競う | try to win against others | We compete on quality, not price. || Three cafes on one street compete for the same customers. || I'm not competing with anyone but myself. | business | compete with / against 人、compete for 賞やお客。名詞は competition。
complete | v | 完了する | finish | Please complete the form. || I completed my first half marathon. || Coffee and a sunset. My day is complete. | paperwork | 形容詞でも使う（a complete mess）。会話の「終わらせる」は finish の方が多い。
concentrate | v | 集中する | focus your mind | I can't concentrate with this noise. || I concentrate better at night. || Concentrate on your form, not the weight. | study | concentrate on ~ の形。focus on とほぼ同じ。
consider | v | 検討する・考慮する | think about carefully | We'll consider your proposal. || Have you considered moving to Tokyo? || I consider him family. | business | consider + -ing（〜することを検討する）。consider to do は間違い。
contact | v | 連絡する | get in touch with | Feel free to contact me anytime. || How can I contact the venue? || Let's stay in contact after the tour. | business | contact 人（to は不要）。contact to me は間違い。
continue | v | 続ける | keep going | Let's continue tomorrow. || The rain continued all night. || If the pain continues, see a doctor. | work | continue to do / continue -ing の両方OK。会話では keep -ing が多い。
create | v | 作り出す | make something new | I create music and videos. || We created a new drink for summer. || Don't create drama at work. | music | create content / create drama。make より「新しく生み出す」感じ。
damage | v | 傷つける・損害を与える | harm or break | The rain damaged my shoes. || Loud music can damage your hearing. || The scandal damaged the brand. | daily | 名詞の damage は数えない（a damage ではない）。物にも評判にも使う。
decide | v | 決める | make a choice | I can't decide what to order. || We decided to start our own label. || Let me know when you decide. | food | decide to do の形。大小どんな決断にも使える。
deliver | v | 届ける・やり遂げる | bring / do what you promised | We always deliver on time. || Do you deliver to this area? || The band really delivered tonight. | business | 「期待に応える・結果を出す」の意味でも使う。
deny | v | 否定する | say something isn't true | He denied everything. || I won't deny it. I ate your cake. || The bank denied my loan application. | conflict | deny + -ing（〜したことを否定する）。「拒否する」の意味にもなる。
depend | v | 次第である・頼る | be decided by / rely on | It depends on the weather. || We depend on our regular customers. || Depends. Who's paying? | daily | 返事の It depends. / Depends. は「場合による」の定番。depend on ~ の形。
describe | v | 説明する・描写する | say what something is like | Describe your perfect weekend. || How would you describe your music? || I can't describe how nervous I was. | daily | describe 物 to 人。describe about は間違い。
destroy | v | 破壊する・ボコボコにする | ruin completely | The new update destroyed my battery. || We destroyed them in the dance battle. || That breakup destroyed me. | online | スラングで「圧勝する」「精神的にやられる」の意味でも使う。
develop | v | 開発する・発達させる | build or grow | We're developing a new app. || I developed a taste for black coffee. || The story develops slowly, but it's worth it. | business | develop a habit / develop a skill のように「身につける」にも使う。
disagree | v | 反対する | have a different opinion | I respectfully disagree. || We disagree about almost everything, but we're still friends. || Spicy food disagrees with me. | conflict | disagree with ~ の形。食べ物が disagrees with me は「体に合わない」。
discover | v | 発見する | find for the first time | I discovered this band last week. || I discovered a great cafe in the alley behind the station. || Discover your own sound. | music | 初めて見つける。find より「発見」のワクワク感がある。
discuss | v | 話し合う | talk about | Let's discuss it over lunch. || We need to discuss the setlist. || Can we discuss my schedule? | work | 他動詞なので discuss about は間違い。talk about なら about が付く。
escape | v | 逃げる・抜け出す | get away | I need to escape the city this weekend. || Music is my escape. || We escaped the rain by ducking into a bar. | travel | 名詞の escape は「逃げ場・息抜き」。escape from ~ の形も。
exchange | v | 交換する・両替する | give and receive | Can I exchange this for a bigger size? || Where can I exchange dollars for yen? || We exchanged numbers after the show. | shopping | exchange A for B（AをBと交換）。両替にも使う。
exist | v | 存在する | be real | Does a perfect job even exist? || That cafe doesn't exist anymore. || I forgot my ex existed until today. | feelings | 会話では There is ~ の方が普通。exist は少し大げさ・哲学っぽい響き。
forget | v | 忘れる | not remember | Don't forget your charger. || I forgot to text her back. || Forget it. It's not important. | daily | forget to do（〜し忘れる）と forget -ing（〜したことを忘れる）は違う。
gain | v | 得る・増える | get / increase | I gained five kilos in Osaka. Worth it. || We gained two hundred followers after the livestream. || You've got nothing to lose and everything to gain. | food | gain weight（太る）、gain followers / experience。
guarantee | v | 保証する | promise something will happen | I can't guarantee it'll work. || All our beans come with a freshness guarantee. || I guarantee you'll love this ramen. | business | I guarantee ~（絶対〜だよ）は会話でも使う強い約束。
hate | v | 大嫌いである | strongly dislike | I hate waking up early. || I hate it when people talk during a show. || Don't hate me, but I don't like sushi. | daily | hate it when ~（〜されるのが嫌）。Don't hate me, but ~ は前置きの定番。
heal | v | 癒える・治る | become healthy again | Time heals everything. Mostly. || My knee hasn't healed yet. || Music helped me heal after the breakup. | health | 傷が治るは heal、病気が治るは get better / recover が自然。
hide | v | 隠す・隠れる | put out of sight | Where did you hide the snacks? || I hide in the back room when the cafe gets crazy. || Don't hide your feelings. | daily | 過去形 hid、過去分詞 hidden。hidden gem（隠れた名店）も便利。
hurt | v | 痛む・傷つける | cause pain | My legs hurt from leg day. || That comment really hurt. || It doesn't hurt to ask. | gym | 過去形も hurt。It doesn't hurt to ~（〜しても損はない）は定番。
imagine | v | 想像する | picture in your mind | Imagine living in New York. || I can't imagine life without music. || Imagine selling out this place. Someday. | feelings | imagine + -ing の形。I imagine ~ は「〜だろうね」と推測する言い方。
include | v | 含む | have as part | Does the price include tax? || Breakfast is included. || Include me in the group chat. | money | be included（含まれている）。including ~（〜を含めて）も頻出。
influence | v | 影響を与える | change how someone thinks | Music influences my mood a lot. || Who influenced your style the most? || Don't let other people influence your decisions. | music | 名詞も同じ形（a big influence on me）。influence on の on に注意。
inform | v | 知らせる | tell officially | We'll inform you by email. || Please inform your manager if you're sick. || Thanks for informing me. | business | tell より硬いビジネス語。inform 人 of / about ~ の形。
inspire | v | 刺激する・やる気を与える | make someone want to do | Your story really inspired me. || This city inspires a lot of my songs. || What inspired you to open a cafe? | feelings | inspire 人 to do。「インスパイアされた」は I was inspired by ~。
involve | v | 伴う・巻き込む | include as a necessary part | The job involves a lot of travel. || I don't want to get involved in their drama. || Being a barista involves a lot of standing. | work | get involved in ~（〜に関わる）。involve + -ing の形も。
join | v | 参加する | become part of | Want to join us for dinner? || I joined a gym near my place. || Join the waitlist and we'll text you. | friends | 他動詞なので join to us は間違い。join a club / join us。
lose | v | 失う・負ける | not have anymore / not win | I lost my keys again. || We lost the game, but it was fun. || I always lose track of time in the studio. | daily | lose track of time（時間を忘れる）、lose weight（やせる）。loose（ゆるい）とつづり注意。
maintain | v | 維持する | keep in good condition | It's hard to maintain a streak. || Lifting helps me maintain my weight. || Maintaining a car in the city costs a lot. | study | keep より硬いが、体型や機械の維持の話で頻出。
measure | v | 測る | find the size or amount | How do you measure success? || Measure the milk before you steam it. || I measured the room for the new desk. | business | 長さや量を測る。体重は weigh myself、体温は take my temperature が自然。
overcome | v | 乗り越える | succeed despite problems | You can overcome anything with time. || I'm still trying to overcome my stage fright. || She overcame a lot to get where she is. | faith | overcome a fear / a challenge が定番。過去形は overcame。
own | v | 所有する | have as your property | I don't own a car. || My uncle owns three restaurants in Osaka. || Own your mistakes. | money | own your mistakes（自分の非を認める）のような比喩も人気。my own は「自分自身の」。
participate | v | 参加する | take part | Everyone needs to participate. || Do you want to participate in the open mic? || Thanks to everyone who participated in the survey. | study | participate in の in を忘れない。会話では join / take part の方が多い。
perform | v | 演奏する・行う | play music on stage / do | We're performing live on Friday. || The new barista is performing really well. || I perform better with a small crowd. | music | 演奏する・演じるのほか「成果を出す」の意味にも使う。
predict | v | 予測する | say what will happen | Nobody can predict the market. || I predict this song will blow up. || Can you predict how busy we'll be tonight? | money | 名詞は prediction。天気や数字の予測は forecast も使う。
prepare | v | 準備する | get ready | I need to prepare for the interview. || We prepare the pastries the night before. || Prepare yourself. The boss is in a mood. | work | prepare for ~（〜に備える）と prepare ~（〜を用意する）の違いに注意。
prevent | v | 防ぐ | stop from happening | Stretching prevents injury. || Wear earplugs to prevent hearing damage. || What can we do to prevent this from happening again? | gym | prevent A from -ing（Aが〜するのを防ぐ）の形を覚える。
produce | v | 制作する・生産する | make (music, goods) | He produces beats for rappers. || Who produced this track? || The farm produces most of the vegetables we use. | music | 名詞の produce は「農産物」で、アクセントが前に来る。
protect | v | 守る | keep safe | Protect your energy. || Protect your ears at concerts. || My big brother always protected me. | feelings | protect A from B（BからAを守る）。protective（過保護な）も便利。
provide | v | 提供する | give something needed | We provide free Wi-Fi. || The church provides free meals on Sundays. || Please provide your ID at check-in. | business | provide A with B / provide B for A の2つの形がある。give より硬め。
publish | v | 出版する・公開する | make public | I published my first article. || We published the new menu on our website. || They published a study on sleep and memory. | online | 本や記事だけでなく、ネット上の公開にも使う。
recover | v | 回復する | get better | I'm still recovering from the weekend. || My voice hasn't recovered since the show. || The cafe recovered after a rough winter. | health | recover from ~（〜から回復する）。失った物を取り戻す意味もある。
reduce | v | 減らす | make smaller | Try to reduce sugar. || We reduced the price of lattes to compete. || Lifting reduces my stress. | health | reduce A to B（AをBに減らす）。cut back on ~ はくだけた言い方。
relax | v | くつろぐ | rest and feel calm | Relax. It's not that deep. || I relax by playing guitar after work. || Relax your shoulders when you sing. | feelings | 形容詞は relaxed。I'm relax. は間違いで I'm relaxed. と言う。
remember | v | 覚えている・思い出す | keep in memory | Do you remember me? || Remember to lock the door. || I don't remember saying that. | friends | remember to do（忘れずに〜する）と remember -ing（〜したのを覚えている）は違う。
remove | v | 取り除く | take away | Please remove your shoes. || Can you remove my name from the list? || This stain won't come out. How do I remove it? | daily | take off / take out より硬い。ネットの投稿を消すのも remove。
repair | v | 修理する | fix | Can you repair my phone screen? || The shop repaired my guitar in a day. || We need to repair the roof before the rainy season. | shopping | 会話では fix の方が多い。repair は専門的な修理のイメージ。
repeat | v | 繰り返す | say or do again | Could you repeat that, please? || Don't repeat this to anyone. || I've had this song on repeat all week. | study | on repeat（リピート再生で）。repeat again は意味が重複するので避ける。
replace | v | 交換する・取って代わる | put something new instead | AI won't replace you. Someone using AI might. || We need to replace the coffee grinder. || Nobody can replace you on bass. | online | replace A with B（AをBに替える）。with の位置に注意。
require | v | 必要とする | need officially | The job requires English. || The venue requires a deposit. || Most visas require a lot of paperwork. | work | need より硬く、規則や条件の話に多い。required（必須の）も頻出。
respect | v | 尊敬する・尊重する | admire / treat well | I respect your honesty. || Respect the gym. Put your weights back. || I don't agree with him, but I respect him. | friends | with all due respect（お言葉ですが）は反論前の定番。
respond | v | 返答する・反応する | answer | She hasn't responded yet. || How did the crowd respond to the new song? || Please respond by Friday. | dm | respond to ~ の to を忘れない。reply より「反応する」の意味が広い。
rest | v | 休む | stop and relax | Rest day today. || Rest your voice before the show. || I'll do the rest. Go home. | gym | the rest は「残り」。rest day（休養日）はジムの定番。
retire | v | 引退する | stop working forever | I'm never retiring. I'll just get weirder. || My boss retired and handed me the shop. || I'm retiring this hoodie. It's seen enough. | work | 物を「お役御免にする」冗談にも使える。retire from ~。
reveal | v | 明かす | show something hidden | The trailer revealed too much. || She revealed she's been writing songs for years. || We'll reveal the album cover on Friday. | online | reveal a secret / reveal the cover。SNSの「解禁」にも使える。
ruin | v | 台無しにする | destroy, spoil | You ruined the ending for me! || The rain ruined our outdoor show. || Don't let one bad customer ruin your day. | online | ruin someone's day / ruin the mood が定番。
save | v | 救う・貯める・保存する | rescue / keep money / store | Save your work before it crashes. || I'm saving for a new mic. || Thanks, you saved my life. | online | save for ~（〜のために貯める）。You saved my life. は大げさな「助かった」。
search | v | 探す・検索する | look for | I searched everywhere. || I searched for that song for weeks. || Search our name and you'll find us. | online | search for ~（〜を探す）。search 場所 は「場所の中を探す」なので語順に注意。
serve | v | 提供する・仕える | give food / help | Breakfast is served until 10. || I served over two hundred customers today. || We serve the best cold brew in Osaka. | food | serve customers / serve food。「仕える」の意味で serve God とも言う。
shake | v | 振る・揺れる | move quickly back and forth | My hands were shaking before the show. || Shake the bottle before you pour. || Let's shake on it. | music | shake hands（握手する）、Let's shake on it.（握手で決めよう）。
steal | v | 盗む | take without permission | Someone stole my bike. || Can I steal you for a minute? || That jacket was a steal at ten bucks. | daily | Can I steal you for a sec?（ちょっといい？）、It's a steal.（激安）は会話で便利。
suffer | v | 苦しむ | feel pain | No need to suffer in silence. || Sales suffered during the rainy season. || I suffer from bad migraines. | health | suffer from ~（〜に苦しむ・〜を患う）の形が多い。
support | v | 支える・応援する | help / encourage | Thanks for supporting my music. || My family supports my dream. || Support local businesses. | music | 日本語の「サポート（手伝う）」より「応援する・支持する」の意味が強い。
switch | v | 切り替える | change from one to another | Let's switch seats. || Can I switch shifts with you on Friday? || I switched to oat milk last year. | daily | switch to ~（〜に切り替える）。swap はくだけた「交換する」。
taste | v | 味がする | have a flavor | This tastes amazing. || Can I taste the sauce? || You have great taste in music. | food | taste + 形容詞（tastes good）。名詞の taste は「好み・センス」の意味も。
tend to | v | 〜しがちである | usually do | I tend to talk too fast when I'm nervous. || Customers tend to order more on rainy days. || I tend to overthink everything. | feelings | tend to + 動詞の原形。「〜しがち」を自然に言える便利な表現。
threaten | v | 脅す | say you'll hurt someone | Are you threatening me? || The landlord threatened to raise the rent. || Rain is threatening to cancel the festival. | conflict | threaten to do（〜すると脅す）。天気などが「〜しそう」の意味にもなる。
tolerate | v | 我慢する | accept something unpleasant | I can't tolerate rude customers. || We don't tolerate harassment at this venue. || I can tolerate a lot, but not bad coffee. | work | 会話では put up with の方が多い。名詞 tolerance は「耐性」。
trigger | v | 引き起こす | start a reaction | Loud noises trigger my headaches. || That song triggers so many memories. || Don't trigger him. He's had a long day. | health | SNSでは triggered（過剰反応した・イラついた）というスラングも。
upgrade | v | アップグレードする | make better | I upgraded my plan. || They upgraded us to business class for free. || I upgraded my home studio with new monitors. | online | get upgraded（無料でアップグレードされる）。名詞でも使う。
vote | v | 投票する | choose in an election | Did you vote? || Let's vote on the band name. || I vote for pizza. | daily | vote for ~（〜に投票する）。I vote for ~ は「〜に一票」の軽い提案にも使う。
warn | v | 警告する | tell about danger | I warned you about the spicy sauce. || The app warned me my password was weak. || Fair warning, my roommate is a little weird. | food | warn 人 about ~ / warn 人 not to do。Fair warning,（先に言っとくけど）も便利。
wonder if | phr | 〜かなと思う | ask yourself politely | I was wondering if you're free Friday. || I wonder if the cafe is still open. || I was wondering if you could cover my shift. | crush | I was wondering if ~ は丁寧なお願いやお誘いの定番。過去進行形で柔らかくなる。
// Feelings & describing — the everyday adjectives
afraid | adj | 怖い・恐れている | scared | I'm afraid of heights. || I'm afraid we're out of oat milk. || Don't be afraid to ask questions. | feelings | I'm afraid ~ は「残念ながら」の丁寧な前置き。名詞の前には置かない。
alive | adj | 生きている | living | I feel so alive after a workout. || The city comes alive at night. || My old guitar is somehow still alive. | gym | come alive（活気づく）。名詞の前では a living thing のように living を使う。
alone | adj | 一人で | without others | I like eating alone sometimes. || Leave me alone. I'm working. || You're not alone. We all feel that way. | daily | alone は「一人で」で中立。lonely は「寂しい」で気持ちを表す。
angry | adj | 怒った | mad | Why are you angry at me? || I get angry when I'm hungry. || Customers get angry when the line is slow. | conflict | angry at / with 人、angry about 事。会話では mad もよく使う。
brave | adj | 勇敢な | not afraid | That was brave. || It was brave of you to sing your own song. || I'm not brave enough to try the spiciest one. | feelings | It's brave of you to ~ の形が便利。courageous は硬め。
bright | adj | 明るい・頭がいい | full of light / smart | She's really bright. || Turn down the screen. It's too bright. || The future looks bright for the shop. | study | 人に使うと「頭がいい」。色なら bright red（鮮やかな赤）。
