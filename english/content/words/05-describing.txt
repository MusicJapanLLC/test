// Describing things — adjectives you hear every day
amazing | adj | すごい・最高の | very good | This view is amazing. || You made this beat in an hour? That's amazing. || The staff at that tiny hotel were amazing. | daily | もともと強い語なので very amazing とは言わない。absolutely amazing や so amazing が自然。
awesome | adj | 最高の | very good (casual) | Awesome, see you then! || You got us a table? Awesome. || The new gym has an awesome view of the river. | friends | 「いいね・了解」の相づちにも使う。仕事メールなら great の方が無難。

disgusting | adj | 気持ち悪い・ひどい | very unpleasant | The bathroom was disgusting. || Some guy spit on the platform. Disgusting. || Racist comments in my chat are disgusting. I ban them right away. | daily | 見た目や味だけでなく、行動を強く非難する時にも使う。gross より強い。
cute | adj | かわいい | attractive in a sweet way | Your dog is so cute. || That little cafe on the corner is so cute. || You think she's cute? Go talk to her. | daily | 人には「かわいい・感じがいい」。小物や店にも使える。
gorgeous | adj | 超きれいな | very beautiful | The sunset was gorgeous. || Your girlfriend looks gorgeous tonight. || The church has a gorgeous old organ. | travel | very beautiful より強い褒め言葉。人・景色・物に使える。

quiet | adj | 静かな | with little noise | Is there a quiet café near here? || Keep it quiet. The baby's sleeping. || Business is quiet on rainy days. | cafe | 「静か」だけでなく、店が「暇・客が少ない」にも使える。

expensive | adj | 高い（値段） | costing a lot | Tokyo is expensive. || Rent near the station is so expensive. || Cheap gear ends up being expensive. | money | price（価格）には high を使い、物には expensive。The price is expensive. は不自然。
cheap | adj | 安い | not costing much | The tacos were cheap and great. || Cheap headphones break fast. || Flights are cheap if you book early. | money | 「安っぽい」の悪い響きもあるので、褒めるなら affordable や a good price。

uncomfortable | adj | 居心地が悪い | not relaxed | That question made me uncomfortable. || These chairs are so uncomfortable. || Sorry if I made you uncomfortable. | feelings | 物の「座り心地が悪い」と、人の「居心地が悪い・気まずい」の両方。
dangerous | adj | 危険な | could hurt you | That road is dangerous at night. || Lifting heavy without a spotter is dangerous. || It's dangerous to sign a contract you don't understand. | travel | It's dangerous to ~ の形。risky は「リスクがある」でもう少し軽い。

fresh | adj | 新鮮な・爽やかな | new, not old | Fresh bread smells amazing. || The bread's fresh out of the oven. || Get some fresh air. You've been in the studio all day. | food | fresh air、fresh start（再出発）など食べ物以外にも幅広く使う。
stale | adj | 古くなった | no longer fresh | The chips are stale. || Don't serve stale croissants. Toss them. || The joke got stale after the third time. | food | パンやお菓子の「湿気た・古くなった」。ネタやアイデアが「新鮮味がない」にも。
huge | adj | 巨大な・すごい | very big / very important | That's huge news! || I'm a huge fan of your work. || Getting that client was huge for us. | daily | 大きさだけでなく「重大な・大事な」。huge fan は「大ファン」。
tiny | adj | 小さい | very small | My apartment is tiny. || The kitchen is tiny, but we make it work. || There's a tiny scratch on the screen. | daily | very small の口語。teeny-tiny は「ちっちゃい」の可愛い言い方。
massive | adj | でかい | very large | They have a massive TV. || We got a massive order from a hotel. || That bass sounds massive on big speakers. | daily | huge とほぼ同じ。イギリス英語や若者言葉でよく聞く。
quick | adj | 速い・手短な | fast, short | Quick question. || Can I grab a quick coffee before we start? || She's a quick learner. | work | fast はスピード、quick は「短時間でさっと」。Quick question. は質問の前置き。
