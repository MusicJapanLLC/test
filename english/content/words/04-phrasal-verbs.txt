// Phrasal verbs — the backbone of spoken English (and listening)
figure out | v | 解決する・理解する | understand or solve by thinking | I can't figure out this app. || Give me a minute. I'll figure it out. || I'm still figuring out what I want to do with my life. | daily | 目的語が代名詞なら figure it out と間に挟む。understand より「自分で考えて解く」感じ。
find out | v | 知る・調べてわかる | learn a fact | I found out he's from Osaka too. || Find out what time the shop opens. || My mom found out about my tattoo from a photo online. | daily | find は物を見つける、find out は情報や事実を知る。
turn out | v | 結局〜になる | end up being | It turned out to be a great day. || The new guy turned out to be a great drummer. || It turns out the cafe was closed for a private event. | daily | It turns out (that) ~ で「実は〜だった」。turned out to be ~ の形も定番。
end up | v | 結局〜する羽目になる | finally be in a situation | We ended up staying till 3 a.m. || If you skip warm-ups, you'll end up injured. || I ended up working a double shift. | friends | end up -ing / end up + 場所 の形。予定外の結果に使う。
show up | v | 現れる・来る | arrive, appear | He didn't show up to the meeting. || Only five people showed up to the open mic. || Just show up on time. That's half the job. | work | come の口語で「顔を出す」感じ。Thanks for showing up. のようにも使う。
come up | v | （話題・問題が）出てくる | appear, be mentioned | Something came up. Can we reschedule? || If anything comes up, call me. || Your name came up in the meeting. | work | Something came up. は予定キャンセルの決まり文句で、理由をぼかせる。
come up with | v | 思いつく | think of an idea | She came up with a great name. || We need to come up with a name for the new blend. || Who came up with this idea? | business | think of に近いが、アイデアや解決策を「ひねり出す」感じ。
come across | v | 出くわす・〜な印象を与える | find by chance / seem | You come across as confident. || I came across your music on a playlist. || Don't come across as too pushy in the email. | business | come across as ~ で「〜という印象を与える」。偶然見つける意味もある。
come back | v | 戻る | return | I'll come back later. || Come back anytime. We're open till ten. || That sound is coming back. Everyone's using it again. | daily | 流行が「再ブーム」になる時にも使う。名詞は comeback。
come over | v | 家に来る | visit someone's home | Wanna come over tonight? || My parents are coming over for dinner. || Come over after your shift. I'll cook. | friends | come over は「家に来る」。相手の家に行くのは go over to your place。
come on | int | ほら・頼むよ・まさか | hurry, encourage, or disbelief | Come on, we're late! || Come on, one more rep! || Oh, come on. You said you'd be here at eight. | friends | 「頑張れ」「早く」「勘弁してよ」と口調で意味が変わる。
go on | v | 続ける・起こる | continue / happen | Go on, I'm listening. / What's going on? || What's going on with the Wi-Fi? || Sorry, go on. What were you saying? | daily | What's going on? は「どうしたの？」。go on -ing は「〜し続ける」。
go out | v | 出かける・付き合う | leave home for fun / date | Are you two going out? || Let's go out for drinks after the show. || How long have you two been going out? | crush | go out with 人 で「付き合う」。go out だけなら「出かける」。
go over | v | 確認する・見直す | review | Let's go over the plan one more time. || Can we go over the numbers before the meeting? || Let me go over your lyrics once more. | work | review の口語。go over the details / the plan が定番。
go through | v | 経験する・目を通す | experience / check carefully | He's going through a tough time. || Go through your receipts before tax season. || Every new cafe goes through a slow month. | feelings | go through a hard time（つらい時期を過ごす）と「ひと通り目を通す」の2つ。
go ahead | v | どうぞ・進める | proceed | Go ahead, take the last one. || Go ahead and start without me. || If you want the mic, go ahead. | daily | Go ahead. は許可の「どうぞ」。go ahead and ~ は「先に〜しちゃって」。
get along | v | 仲良くやる | have a good relationship | Do you get along with your boss? || My cat and my roommate don't get along. || We get along great in the studio. | work | get along with 人 の形。大人同士の関係にも自然に使える。
get over | v | 乗り越える・立ち直る | recover from | It took me a year to get over her. || You'll get over it. Give it time. || I can't get over how good this coffee is. | crush | can't get over ~ は「〜がすごすぎて信じられない」という意味にもなる。
get up | v | 起きる | leave bed | I get up at six for the gym. || I have to get up at four for the morning shift. || Get up. We're going to the gym. | daily | wake up は目が覚める、get up は起き上がる。順番は wake up → get up。
get back | v | 戻る・取り戻す | return / receive again | When did you get back from Tokyo? || I just got back from the gym. || Did you get your deposit back? | travel | get back from ~（戻る）と get ~ back（取り戻す）の2つの形。
get back to | v | 折り返し連絡する | reply later | I'll get back to you tomorrow. || Let me check and get back to you. || Sorry, I never got back to you about Friday. | business | 仕事でも友達でも使える「折り返す・あとで返事する」の定番。
get rid of | v | 処分する・取り除く | throw away / remove | I need to get rid of these old clothes. || We got rid of the old espresso machine. || How do I get rid of this headache? | daily | 物を捨てる以外に、痛み・習慣・人を「追い払う」にも使える。
get away with | v | 罰されずに済む | not be punished for | You won't get away with this. || He thinks he can get away with being late every day. || Don't let him get away with it. | conflict | get away with -ing の形で「〜してもお咎めなし」。
get by | v | なんとかやっていく | manage with little | I get by with basic English. || We can get by on one income for now. || How do you get by on four hours of sleep? | money | get by on ~ の形。「なんとか暮らす・やっていく」。
get in | v | 乗る・入る | enter a car or place | Get in, we're late. || Get in. I'll drive you to the station. || What time did you get in last night? | travel | 車やタクシーは get in、電車やバスは get on。「帰宅する・到着する」の意味もある。
get out | v | 出る・降りる | leave | Get out of the car. || Get out of here! You won the contest? || Let's get out of the city this weekend. | travel | Get out of here! は「マジで！？」という驚きにもなる。口調に注意。
get on | v | （電車・バスに）乗る | board | Get on the train at Umeda. || I'll get on the train at Namba. || Get on the next bus. It's faster. | travel | 電車・バス・飛行機・自転車は get on。車やタクシーは get in。
get off | v | 降りる | leave a bus or train | Get off at the next stop. || Get off the train at Shin-Osaka. || What time do you get off work? | travel | get off work（仕事が終わる）も超よく使う。
get into | v | ハマる・入る | become interested in | I got into jazz last year. || How did you get into making beats? || Get into the habit of saving your files. | music | How did you get into ~? は趣味の始まりを聞く定番。get into the habit of ~ は「〜を習慣にする」。
give up | v | 諦める・やめる | stop trying | Don't give up on your English. || I gave up sugar for a month. || Never give up on the people you love. | study | give up on ~（見切りをつける）、give up -ing（やめる）の形。
give back | v | 返す | return | Give me back my phone! || I want to give back to the neighborhood that helped me. || Give back my hoodie when you're done. | daily | give back to ~ で「（地域や社会に）恩返しする」。
give in | v | 折れる・屈する | stop resisting | I gave in and bought the shoes. || Don't give in to the haters. || My kid cried until I gave in. | shopping | give in to ~ の形。誘惑やプレッシャーに「負ける・折れる」。
hang up | v | 電話を切る | end a phone call | Don't hang up! || She hung up on me in the middle of a sentence. || Let me hang up. My boss is calling. | phone | hang up on 人 で「ガチャ切りする」。
hold on | v | 待つ | wait | Hold on, let me check. || Hold on a sec. I'm writing this down. || Hold on tight. The bus is packed. | phone | 電話や会話の「ちょっと待って」。Wait! より柔らかい。
look after | v | 世話をする | take care of | Can you look after my cat this weekend? || Who's looking after the shop while you're away? || Look after yourself. You've been working too much. | daily | take care of とほぼ同じ。look after yourself は別れ際の「体に気をつけて」にも。
look for | v | 探す | try to find | I'm looking for a cheap gym. || We're looking for a part-time barista. || What are you looking for? Maybe I can help. | daily | 探している最中は look for、見つけたら find。
look forward to | v | 楽しみにする | be excited about | I'm looking forward to the trip. || I'm looking forward to seeing you at church. || Looking forward to working with you. | travel | to の後は名詞か -ing。look forward to see は超定番のミス。
look into | v | 調べる | investigate | I'll look into it and get back to you. || We're looking into opening on Sundays. || Can you look into why the card reader keeps failing? | business | 調査・検討の言い方。仕事の I'll look into it. はとても便利。
look out | v | 気をつける | be careful | Look out! A bike! || Look out for pickpockets near the station. || Look out, the floor's wet. | daily | look out for ~ で「〜に気をつける」。叫ぶなら Watch out! も同じ。
look up | v | 調べる | search for information | Look it up online. || I looked up the lyrics after the show. || Things are looking up for the shop. | study | look ~ up で「調べる」。Things are looking up. は「状況が良くなってきた」。
look up to | v | 尊敬する | admire | I look up to my older brother. || The younger guys at the gym look up to you. || Who did you look up to as a kid? | feelings | look down on（見下す）の反対。respect より口語的。
make up | v | 仲直りする・でっち上げる | become friends again / invent | They fought but made up. / He made up an excuse. || Let's make up. I hate fighting. || Don't make up numbers in the report. | conflict | 「仲直り」「でっち上げ」「化粧（makeup）」と意味が多い。make up for ~ は「埋め合わせる」。
make it | v | 間に合う・成功する・来られる | arrive in time / succeed | Can you make it to the party? || We made it! The flight hasn't left. || Sorry, I can't make it tonight. | friends | I can't make it. は誘いを断る定番。make it big は「大成功する」。
make out | v | 理解する・いちゃつく | understand / kiss for a long time | I can't make out what he's saying. || I can't make out the words on this sign. || They were making out in the back of the bus. | crush | 「判読する」と「いちゃつく」という全然違う意味。文脈に注意。
pick up | v | 拾う・迎えに行く・覚える | lift / collect / learn | I'll pick you up at seven. / You'll pick up English fast. || Can you pick up some milk on the way? || I picked up a few Spanish words at work. | daily | 「拾う」「迎えに行く」「買ってくる」「自然に覚える」と幅広い。pick up the phone は「電話に出る」。
pick out | v | 選ぶ | choose | Help me pick out a shirt. || She picked out a nice shirt for my show. || Pick out any cake you want. It's on me. | shopping | たくさんの中から「選び出す」。choose より日常的。
put off | v | 先延ばしにする | delay | Stop putting off the dentist. || I keep putting off my taxes. || Don't put off the call. Just do it. | health | put off -ing の形。procrastinate（先延ばしにする）の口語版。
put up with | v | 我慢する | tolerate | I can't put up with the noise anymore. || How do you put up with that noise every night? || I put up with a lot for this job. | conflict | tolerate の口語。I can't stand it. も「我慢できない」でよく使う。
put on | v | 着る・つける | wear / turn on | Put on a jacket. It's cold. || Put on some music while we clean. || I put on three kilos over the holidays. | daily | 「着る」の他、音楽をかける・体重が増える（put on weight）にも。着ている状態は wear。
run into | v | ばったり会う | meet by chance | I ran into my ex at the supermarket. || Did you run into anyone interesting at the party? || We ran into some problems with the payment system. | daily | 人にばったり会う以外に、run into problems（問題にぶつかる）も定番。
run out of | v | 使い切る・切らす | have no more | We ran out of coffee. || We're running out of time. || Don't run out of milk during the morning rush. | daily | 進行形 running out of ~ で「なくなりかけ」。
set up | v | 設定する・準備する・紹介する | arrange / prepare | Can you set up the meeting? || We set up the gear in twenty minutes. || My friend set me up on a blind date. | work | 準備・設定の他に「（人を）紹介してくっつける」の意味もある。名詞は setup。
show-off | n | 目立ちたがり | a person who shows off | Don't be a show-off. || He's doing one-arm pull-ups again. What a show-off. || I'm not a show-off. I just like nice shoes. | friends | 名詞は show-off（ハイフンあり）、動詞は show off（ハイフンなし）。
sort out | v | 解決する・整理する | fix or organize | Let's sort out the details tomorrow. || Let me sort out my receipts first. || Don't worry, we'll sort it out. | work | 問題を「片付ける・解決する」。イギリス英語で特によく聞く。
take off | v | 脱ぐ・離陸する・うまくいき始める | remove / leave the ground / suddenly succeed | Our business really took off this year. || Take off your shoes at the door. || When does your flight take off? | business | 「脱ぐ」「離陸する」「急にブレイクする」の3つ。take a day off は「一日休む」。
take over | v | 引き継ぐ・乗っ取る | take control | Can you take over while I'm out? || I'll take over the register. Go take your break. || Bots are taking over the comments. | work | 「引き継ぐ」と「乗っ取る・占拠する」の2つ。名詞は takeover。
take care of | v | 対応する・世話をする | handle / look after | I'll take care of the bill. || Take care of yourself. || I'll take care of the music. You handle the food. | money | Take care. は別れの挨拶。「対応する・引き受ける」の意味は仕事で便利。
take back | v | 撤回する・返品する | withdraw words / return | I take it back. You're not lazy. || Can I take back these shoes? They don't fit. || Take that back! | conflict | I take it back. で「さっきの発言取り消す」。返品にも使える。
talk into | v | 説得して〜させる | persuade | She talked me into karaoke. || Don't let him talk you into a second drink. || My friend talked me into opening a shop. | friends | talk 人 into -ing の形。persuade より口語的で、押し切られた感じがある。
talk out of | v | 説得してやめさせる | persuade not to | My friend talked me out of quitting. || Can you talk him out of quitting? || I almost bought it, but my wife talked me out of it. | friends | talk 人 out of -ing の形。talk into の反対。
think over | v | よく考える | consider carefully | Let me think it over. || Think it over and tell me tomorrow. || I need to think over the offer before I sign. | business | 代名詞なら think it over。即答を避ける時のクッションになる。
throw away | v | 捨てる | put in the trash | Don't throw away the receipt. || Don't throw away the old mixes. You never know. || We throw away too much bread at closing. | daily | throw out も同じ意味。throw away a chance は「チャンスを棒に振る」。
try on | v | 試着する | wear to check size | Can I try this on? || Try on a size bigger. || Can I try these shoes on? | shopping | 代名詞なら try it on。服や靴専用で、食べ物は try だけ。
turn down | v | 断る・音量を下げる | refuse / lower | She turned down the offer. / Turn the music down. || Turn down the bass. The neighbors are complaining. || I turned down a gig to go to church with my mom. | business | 「断る」と「音量を下げる」の2つ。turn down an offer / a job が定番。
turn up | v | 音量を上げる・現れる | make louder / appear | Turn it up! I love this song. || Turn up the mic a little. || My lost wallet turned up at the cafe. | music | 「音量を上げる」の他に「（なくした物が）出てくる」。
turn on | v | つける | start a machine | Turn on the lights. || Turn on the oven before you open. || Can you turn on subtitles? | daily | 機械やライトを「つける」。名詞の turn-on は「グッとくるポイント」なので注意。
turn off | v | 消す・萎えさせる | stop a machine / make uninterested | Bad breath is a turn-off. || Turn off your phone during the service. || Arrogance is a total turn-off. | crush | 「消す」の他に、名詞 turn-off で「萎える要素」。
wake up | v | 目を覚ます | stop sleeping | I woke up at noon. || Wake up! We're gonna miss the train. || I wake up at 3 p.m. on night-shift days. | daily | wake up は目が覚める、get up は起き上がる。過去形は woke up。
work out | v | 運動する・うまくいく | exercise / succeed | I work out five days a week. / It all worked out. || Don't worry, it'll work out. || I work out before my cafe shift. | gym | 「運動する」「うまくいく」「（答えを）出す」の3つ。名詞は workout。
back up | v | 支持する・バックアップする | support / copy files | Back me up in the meeting. / Back up your files. || My bandmates backed me up when the venue tried to cut our pay. || Back up a little. You're blocking the door. | work | 「味方する」「データを保存する」「後ろに下がる」。名詞は backup。
break down | v | 故障する・泣き崩れる | stop working / lose control emotionally | My car broke down on the highway. || If the machine breaks down again, call the repair guy. || Let me break down the costs for you. | travel | 「故障する」「泣き崩れる」の他、数字を「細かく分けて説明する」にも。名詞は breakdown。
break up | v | 別れる | end a relationship | They broke up last week. || Why did you two break up? || The cops came to break up the fight. | crush | break up with 人 で「〜と別れる」。喧嘩や集まりを「解散させる」意味も。
bring up | v | 話題に出す・育てる | mention / raise a child | Don't bring up politics at dinner. || Don't bring up money on a first date. || I was brought up in a small town near Nara. | conflict | 「話題に出す」と「育てる（be brought up）」の2つ。
call off | v | 中止する | cancel | They called off the game because of rain. || We had to call off the show because of the typhoon. || Call off the search. I found my keys. | daily | cancel の口語。イベント・試合などを「中止にする」。
calm down | v | 落ち着く | become relaxed | Calm down, it's just a test. || Calm down and tell me what happened. || It took a while for the shop to calm down after the rush. | feelings | 怒っている人に Calm down! と言うと逆効果のことも。Take a breath. の方が優しい。
carry on | v | 続ける | continue | Carry on. Don't let me stop you. || Sorry, carry on with what you were saying. || If you carry on like this, you'll burn out. | work | carry on with ~ / carry on -ing の形。carry-on は「機内持ち込み荷物」。
catch up | v | 追いつく・近況を話す | reach the same level / talk about recent news | Let's catch up over coffee. || I need to catch up on sleep this weekend. || You go ahead. I'll catch up. | friends | catch up on ~ は「遅れを取り戻す」、catch up with 人 は「追いつく・近況を話す」。
check out | v | 見てみる・チェックアウトする | look at / leave a hotel | Check out this video. || What time do we have to check out? || Check out the new menu. We added a matcha latte. | online | 「見てみる」と「ホテルを出る」。Check this out! は「これ見て！」。
cheer up | v | 元気づける・元気出す | become or make happier | Cheer up! It's Friday. || Cheer up. The weekend's almost here. || I bought her flowers to cheer her up. | feelings | cheer 人 up で「元気づける」。cheer for は「応援する」で別の意味。
chip in | v | お金を出し合う | contribute money | Let's all chip in for her gift. || Everyone chipped in for the new speakers. || Can I chip in for gas? | money | 少しずつお金を出し合う時の口語。ちょっと手伝う意味もある。
clean up | v | 片付ける | make tidy | Help me clean up after the party. || Clean up your station before you clock out. || The streets got cleaned up after the festival. | daily | clean up after 人 で「〜の後片付けをする」。
cut down on | v | 減らす | reduce | I'm cutting down on sugar. || I'm trying to cut down on energy drinks. || We cut down on paper cups by giving a discount for your own mug. | health | 量を「減らす」。完全にやめるなら quit / cut out。
deal with | v | 対処する | handle | I'll deal with it tomorrow. || I deal with angry customers every day. || How are you dealing with the stress? | work | handle の口語。Deal with it. は「我慢しろ」とキツく聞こえることがある。
