//! Level Up Verbs | Verbs that make you sound precise | levelup
undermine | v | （じわじわ）損なう・台無しにする | weaken something slowly, often from the inside | Skipping sleep undermines all your work at the gym. || Please don't undermine me in front of the client. || Every late reply kind of undermines the trust we built. | work, health | undermine trust / undermine someone's confidence の形でよく使う。sabotage より静かで、気づかないうちに効いてくるイメージ。
thrive | v | 伸び伸び成長する・絶好調である | grow strong and do really well | Some people thrive on pressure, but I just panic. || The cafe is thriving since we started doing late-night hours. || Plants and introverts both thrive with a little space. | work, business | thrive on ~ で「〜を糧に活き活きする」。survive との対比で I'm not just surviving, I'm thriving. は定番の言い回し。
cope | v | （つらいことに）なんとか対処する・耐える | deal with something hard, especially emotionally | How do you cope with night shifts and still make music? || Dark humor is how I cope, honestly. || She's coping better than I expected after the breakup. | feelings, work | cope with ~ が基本。deal with より「心の負担」に焦点がある。coping mechanism（対処法）もポッドキャスト頻出。
outweigh | v | 〜より重要だ・上回る | be more important or bigger than something else | Honestly, the pros definitely outweigh the cons. || For me, the freedom outweighs the lower pay. | business, work | The benefits outweigh the risks. がテンプレ。more important than を一語で言える便利な動詞。
navigate | v | （複雑な状況を）うまく切り抜ける | find your way through something complicated | I'm still learning how to navigate office politics. || Navigating the visa paperwork took me three months. || We're navigating a long-distance thing right now. | work, paperwork | 本来は「道を進む」。今は navigate a situation / a relationship / the system のように抽象的に使うのが普通。
foster | v | （関係・雰囲気を）育む・促す | help something develop over time | We want to foster a chill, no-judgment vibe at open mic nights. || Good managers foster trust instead of fear. | business, work | foster a culture / community / creativity の形が多い。動物や子どもを「一時的に預かって育てる」意味もある。
overlook | v | 見落とす・大目に見る | fail to notice something, or choose to ignore it | I overlooked one zero on the invoice and almost lost a client. || It's easy to overlook how much the night crew does. || I'll overlook it this time, but don't make it a habit. | work, business | 「見渡す」より「見落とす」が会話では主流。miss より「重要なのに」のニュアンスが強い。
dismiss | v | （意見・気持ちを）軽くあしらう・退ける | treat something as not worth thinking about | He dismissed my idea before I even finished explaining it. || Don't dismiss your feelings just because other people have it worse. | work, feelings | dismiss an idea / a concern / someone's feelings。ignore（無視）と違い「聞いたうえで切り捨てる」。
procrastinate | v | （やるべきことを）先延ばしにする | keep delaying something you should do | I procrastinate by cleaning my room, which is honestly kind of productive. || Stop procrastinating and just send the demo. | study, work | put off より習慣的で自覚がある感じ。名詞 procrastination もセットで覚えると便利。
commit | v | 本気で取り組むと決める・コミットする | promise yourself fully to something | If you commit to three gym days a week, you'll see a difference. || I'm not ready to commit to a relationship right now. || Once you commit to the bit, you can't stop halfway. | gym, crush | commit to のあとは名詞か -ing。commit to going（○）commit to go（×）がよくあるミス。
enforce | v | （規則を）徹底させる・取り締まる | make sure people actually follow a rule | We have a no-phones rule at practice, but nobody enforces it. || The landlord suddenly started enforcing the quiet hours. | work, business | ルールは「作る」より「守らせる」が大変。enforce a rule / a policy / a deadline。
retain | v | 保持する・（人を）引き止める | keep something or someone | I retain more from podcasts than from textbooks. || It's cheaper to retain a regular than to find a new customer. | business, study | keep のフォーマル版。ビジネスでは retain customers / staff（顧客・社員を定着させる）が頻出。
endorse | v | （公に）支持する・推す | publicly say you support someone or something | I only endorse gear I actually use on stream. || Our pastor endorsed the food drive during Sunday service. | business, online | 推薦・スポンサーの文脈で多い。名詞 endorsement（広告契約）。recommend より公的で重い。
mitigate | v | （リスク・被害を）軽減する | make something bad less serious | We back up every session to mitigate the risk of losing a track. || Drinking water won't fully mitigate a hangover, but it helps. | business, work | mitigate the risk / damage / impact。リスクや被害とセットで使う。reduce より専門的に響く。
resent | v | 根に持つ・恨めしく思う | feel bitter about something unfair | I resent having to cover my coworker's shifts every weekend. || Don't say yes if you're gonna resent it later. | feelings, conflict | resent + 名詞 / -ing。発音は「リゼント」。resend（再送）と混同しやすいので注意。
envision | v | 思い描く・構想する | picture something in your mind, usually the future | I envision a small studio above the cafe one day. || How do you envision this working with only two staff? | business, music | imagine より「目標・計画」寄り。envisage はイギリス英語寄りの言い方。
juggle | v | （複数のことを）同時にこなす・やりくりする | handle several things at the same time | I'm juggling two jobs, a side project, and a gym habit. || Juggling night shifts and dating is basically impossible. | work, daily | もとはお手玉。multitask より「必死にバランスを取ってる」感じが出る。
acknowledge | v | （事実・相手を）認める・ちゃんと受け止める | admit or show you recognize something | You don't have to agree, but at least acknowledge how I feel. || He finally acknowledged that the deadline was unrealistic. || The barista acknowledged me with a nod, so I knew my order was coming. | feelings, work | admit より冷静で中立。メールの acknowledge receipt は「受領しました」の定番表現。
anticipate | v | 予期する・見越して備える | expect something and prepare for it | We didn't anticipate such a big crowd for a Tuesday gig. || Good servers anticipate what you need before you ask. | work, business | expect より「先回りして準備」のニュアンス。後ろは -ing（anticipate doing）を取る点にも注意。
advocate | v | （主張・人を）擁護する・推進する | publicly support an idea or a person | I advocate for paying artists, even for small gigs. || You have to advocate for yourself at work, because no one else will. | work, music | advocate for ~ がアメリカで一般的。名詞（擁護者）は語尾が「カット」、動詞は「ケイト」と発音が変わる。
alleviate | v | （痛み・ストレスを）和らげる | make pain or stress less bad | Stretching after leg day really alleviates the soreness. || One more part-timer would alleviate a lot of pressure on the morning crew. | health, work | relieve のやや硬い版。alleviate pain / stress / symptoms。mitigate はリスク寄り、alleviate は苦痛寄り。
allocate | v | （予算・時間を）割り当てる | decide how to share out money, time, or work | We allocated most of the budget to ads this month. || I allocate Sunday mornings to church and Sunday nights to mixing. | business, money | allocate A to B。spend より「計画的に配分する」感じ。名詞 allocation。
articulate | v | （考えを）うまく言葉にする | express an idea clearly in words | I know what I want the song to feel like, I just can't articulate it. || She articulated the problem better than anyone in the meeting. | work, music | explain より「頭の中のもやもやを言語化する」。形容詞（話が明快な）としても使う。
assess | v | 評価する・見極める | judge how good, bad, or serious something is | Let's assess the damage before we panic. || The trainer assessed my form and fixed my squat in five minutes. | work, gym | evaluate とほぼ同じ。assess the situation（状況を見極める）は会話でもよく出る。
attribute | v | （〜の）おかげ・せいだと考える | say something was caused by something else | I attribute my progress to sleep more than to supplements. || She attributes the cafe's success to word of mouth. | business, gym | attribute A to B の形。名詞（属性）とアクセントが違い、動詞は「トリ」が強い。
backfire | v | 裏目に出る | have the opposite result of what you wanted | My plan to look cool totally backfired when I tripped on stage. || Cutting prices backfired, because customers thought the quality dropped. | business, daily | 車のバックファイアから。plan / joke / strategy が主語になる。go wrong より「逆効果」がはっきり。
bond | v | 打ち解ける・絆が深まる | become close to someone through a shared experience | We bonded over our love of terrible horror movies. || Nothing bonds a team like a broken espresso machine at 7 a.m. | friends, work | bond over ~（〜を通じて仲良くなる）が鉄板。get along より「関係が一段深くなる」。
capitalize | v | （チャンスを）活かす・つけ込む | use a chance to get an advantage | We should capitalize on the hype while the video is still trending. || They capitalized on our mistake and took half our customers. | business, online | capitalize on ~ が会話での使い方。「大文字にする」の意味もあるので文脈で判断。
cherish | v | 大切にする・慈しむ | love and value something deeply | I cherish those late-night talks with my grandma. || Cherish the small wins, because the big ones take forever. | feelings, faith | like / love より深く、思い出や人に使う。物を「大事に使う」なら take care of の方が自然。
clarify | v | はっきりさせる・明確にする | make something easier to understand | Can you clarify what you mean by "soon"? || Just to clarify, the price includes mixing and mastering, right? | work, business | Just to clarify, ... は確認の前置きとして超便利。What do you mean? より丁寧で角が立たない。
cling | v | しがみつく・執着する | hold on tightly, physically or emotionally | Stop clinging to that old plan, it's not working. || My cat clings to me every time I try to leave for work. | feelings, daily | cling to ~。過去形は clung。形容詞 clingy（ベタベタする）と同じ語源。
compensate | v | 補う・埋め合わせる・報酬を払う | make up for something, or pay someone | I compensate for my weak voice with really good lyrics. || Will we be compensated for the extra hours? | work, money | compensate for ~（〜を補う）。仕事では「報酬・補償」の意味で受け身がよく使われる。
comply | v | （規則・要求に）従う | follow a rule or request | Every shop has to comply with the new tax rules. || I complied, but I wasn't happy about it. | business, paperwork | comply with ~。follow より公式・法的な響き。名詞 compliance（コンプライアンス）。
concede | v | （しぶしぶ）認める・敗北を認める | admit something is true, often unwillingly | Fine, I'll concede that your mix sounds better than mine. || The other candidate conceded before midnight. | conflict, work | admit より「負けを認める」ニュアンス。I'll concede that ~ は議論で余裕を見せる言い方。
confide | v | （秘密を）打ち明ける | tell someone private things because you trust them | She confided in me that she's thinking about quitting. || I don't usually confide in coworkers, but this guy's different. | friends, feelings | confide in + 人。tell より「信頼して打ち明ける」。名詞 confidant（打ち明け相手）。
confront | v | （問題・人に）向き合う・面と向かって言う | face a problem or a person directly | I finally confronted my roommate about the dishes. || You can't heal if you never confront what happened. | conflict, feelings | 日本語の「対決」ほど激しくないことも多い。confront someone about ~ の形でよく使う。
contemplate | v | じっくり考える・検討する | think deeply about something, often a big decision | I've been contemplating moving to Tokyo for the music scene. || Every night shift around 4 a.m., I contemplate my life choices. | feelings, work | contemplate + -ing。think about より深刻で長い時間。4 a.m. のような自虐ネタでもよく使う。
contradict | v | 矛盾する・反論する | say the opposite of something | Your texts kind of contradict what you said on the date. || Don't contradict me in front of customers, just tell me later. | conflict, crush | 人にも事実にも使う。You're contradicting yourself.（言ってることが矛盾してる）は口論の定番。
curate | v | 厳選して並べる・キュレーションする | carefully choose and organize things | I curate the cafe playlist every week, so blame me if it's weird. || Her feed is so curated, it doesn't even feel real. | music, online | 美術館の curator から。今は playlist / feed / menu を「センスで選ぶ」意味で日常的。
dabble | v | （趣味程度に）ちょっと手を出す | try an activity without being serious | I dabble in photography, but music is my main thing. || He dabbled in crypto and lost a month's rent. | music, money | dabble in ~。try より「本気じゃない」感じ。謙遜にも使える。
deflect | v | （質問・批判を）かわす・そらす | avoid a question or criticism by changing focus | Every time I ask about his ex, he deflects with a joke. || You always deflect compliments, just say thank you. | conflict, crush | 本来は「弾をそらす」。deflect a question / blame / a compliment。dodge よりスマートなかわし方。
derail | v | （計画・会話を）脱線させる・頓挫させる | throw a plan or conversation off course | One bad week shouldn't derail your whole diet. || Sorry to derail the meeting, but is anyone else starving? | work, health | 列車の脱線から。derail a project / a conversation。Sorry to derail ~ は会議で使える。
deter | v | 思いとどまらせる・抑止する | make someone decide not to do something | The rain didn't deter anyone from coming to the show. || Cameras deter shoplifting, mostly. | business, daily | deter A from -ing。stop より「やる気を削ぐ」。名詞 deterrent（抑止力）。
deteriorate | v | 悪化する・劣化する | gradually become worse | My uncle's health deteriorated after years of night shifts. || The conversation deteriorated fast once money came up. | health, conflict | get worse の硬い版。ニュースや医療の話でよく聞く。発音は「ディティアリオレイト」。
diminish | v | 減る・（価値を）下げる | become or make smaller or less important | The pain diminished after a few days of rest. || Don't let anyone diminish what you've achieved. | feelings, health | decrease より「価値・重要性」に使いやすい。diminish someone's achievement（功績を軽んじる）。
disclose | v | （情報を）開示する・打ち明ける | make private information known | You have to disclose sponsored posts, or you can get in trouble. || I'd rather not disclose my salary, thanks. | business, online | reveal よりビジネス・法律寄り。名詞 disclosure。
distract | v | 気を散らす・紛らわす | take someone's attention away from something | My phone distracts me more than any noise. || Stop distracting me, I'm counting the register. || I distract myself with the gym when I'm stressed. | study, work | distract myself with ~（〜で気を紛らわす）も便利。形容詞 distracted（上の空）もセットで。
diversify | v | 多様化する・分散する | add different types to reduce risk or boredom | We diversified the menu so it's not just coffee. || As a musician, you have to diversify your income. | business, money | diversify your income / portfolio。「卵を一つのカゴに盛るな」の発想。
downplay | v | 実際より軽く言う・控えめに扱う | make something seem less important than it is | Don't downplay your injury, just rest for a week. || He downplayed the problem, and now it's twice as big. | health, work | play down とも言う。謙遜しすぎる人に Stop downplaying your skills. と励ませる。
dread | v | （これからのことを）すごく憂うつに思う | feel very worried about something coming | I dread Monday morning inventory. || I'm dreading the conversation with my landlord. | feelings, work | dread + 名詞 / -ing。worry より「来ると分かってて嫌」。I'm dreading it. が自然な会話の形。
dwell | v | （嫌なことを）くよくよ考え続ける | keep thinking about something negative | Don't dwell on one bad set, you'll have a better one next week. || I tend to dwell on stupid things I said years ago. | feelings, music | dwell on ~。overthink と似ているが、過去のことに対して使うことが多い。「住む」は文語的。
elaborate | v | 詳しく説明する | give more details | Can you elaborate on that? || He said "it's complicated" and refused to elaborate. | work, conflict | Can you elaborate? は面接・会議で聞き返す定番。形容詞（凝った）は語尾の発音が「レット」。
embrace | v | 受け入れる・積極的に取り入れる | accept something fully and willingly | I've started to embrace being the weird one in the group. || Small businesses need to embrace new tech or get left behind. | feelings, business | 本来は「抱きしめる」。embrace change / your flaws のように前向きに受け入れる時に使う。
emphasize | v | 強調する | give special importance to something | The coach keeps emphasizing form over weight. || I can't emphasize this enough: back up your files. | gym, work | I can't emphasize this enough は「何度でも言うけど」の決まり文句。emphasize on は×（前置詞不要）。
enhance | v | （質・魅力を）高める | make something better or stronger | A little reverb really enhances the vocals. || The new lighting enhances the whole vibe of the shop. | music, business | improve より「すでに良いものをさらに良く」。改善というより強化。
exceed | v | 上回る・超える | go beyond a number, limit, or expectation | Our sales exceeded our goal for the first time. || The show exceeded all my expectations. | business, work | exceed expectations は評価・レビューの定番。go over より硬い。
exploit | v | 搾取する・（弱点を）突く | use something or someone unfairly for profit | Some labels exploit young artists who don't read contracts. || The hackers exploited a bug in the app. | business, music | 悪い意味が多い。チャンスを「活かす」なら take advantage of や capitalize on が無難。
facilitate | v | 円滑に進める・進行役をする | make a process easier | I facilitate the small group discussion at church on Fridays. || My job is basically to facilitate meetings and keep them short. | work, faith | 会議の進行役 facilitator もこの語。help よりビジネス寄りでフォーマル。
fixate | v | 執着する・こだわりすぎる | think about one thing too much | Stop fixating on the numbers and enjoy the music. || I fixated on one bad comment and ignored fifty good ones. | feelings, online | fixate on ~。obsess とほぼ同じだが、「一点にロックオン」のイメージ。
flourish | v | 繁栄する・花開く | grow or develop very well | The local music scene is flourishing again. || Some kids flourish once they find the right teacher. | music, business | thrive とほぼ同義だが、やや文語的で「花が咲く」比喩がある。
grapple | v | （難題と）格闘する | struggle to deal with a hard problem | I'm still grappling with what I want to do with my life. || The whole industry is grappling with AI right now. | work, feelings | grapple with ~。struggle with より「正面から取っ組み合ってる」感じ。ニュースでよく聞く。
hinder | v | 妨げる・足を引っ張る | make progress slower or harder | Perfectionism hinders more than it helps. || The snow hindered deliveries all week. | work, study | 完全に止めるのではなく「遅らせる・邪魔する」。prevent（防ぐ）との違いに注意。
implement | v | 実行する・導入する | put a plan or system into action | We implemented a points card, and the regulars love it. || Ideas are easy, implementing them is the hard part. | business, work | do より「計画を実際に動かす」。implement a plan / policy / feature は IT・ビジネス頻出。
imply | v | ほのめかす・暗に意味する | suggest something without saying it directly | Are you implying I'm always late? || Her text implied she wasn't interested. | conflict, crush | Are you implying ~? は反撃の定番。imply（話し手がほのめかす）と infer（聞き手が推測する）は逆。
indulge | v | （欲に）ふける・好きなだけ楽しむ | allow yourself to enjoy something extra | It's my birthday, so I'm gonna indulge a little. || I indulge in one cheat meal a week. | food, health | indulge in ~。罪悪感のある楽しみに使う。guilty pleasure と相性がいい。
intervene | v | 介入する・止めに入る | get involved to stop or change a situation | The bouncer intervened before it turned into a fight. || My mom intervened when she saw how little I was sleeping. | conflict, friends | step in の硬い版。ニュースの the government intervened もこの語。
jeopardize | v | 危険にさらす・台無しにしかねない | put something at risk | Don't jeopardize your job over one angry email. || Skipping rest days could jeopardize your progress. | work, health | risk より「失うかもしれない」重さがある。発音は「ジェパダイズ」。
justify | v | 正当化する・正しいと説明する | give a good reason for something | I can't justify spending that much on headphones. || You don't have to justify your choices to everyone. || How do you justify charging extra for oat milk? | money, conflict | can't justify + 名詞 / -ing は「さすがに出せない」という買い物の言い訳に使える。
linger | v | なかなか消えない・居残る | stay longer than expected | The smell of garlic lingered in my car for days. || A few regulars always linger after closing. | daily, cafe | 匂い・感情・人がだらだら残る時に。lingering doubts（消えない疑い）も頻出。
manipulate | v | （人を）操る・（データを）操作する | control someone or something in a sneaky way | He used guilt to manipulate me into staying. || You can't manipulate the algorithm, trust me, I've tried. | crush, online | 人に使うと強い非難。データやソフトを「操作する」意味では中立。
monetize | v | 収益化する | make money from something | I'm finally able to monetize my stream. || Not every hobby needs to be monetized. | online, business | YouTube などの「収益化」はこれ。monetize content / an audience。
neglect | v | おろそかにする・放っておく | not give enough care or attention | I neglected my friends while I was building the business. || Don't neglect your warm-up, your knees will thank you. | friends, gym | forget より「やるべきなのに放置」。名詞としても使える（neglect）。
normalize | v | 当たり前のことにする | make something seem normal or acceptable | We need to normalize taking mental health days. || Let's not normalize answering work emails at midnight. | work, online | SNS で頻出の Normalize ~ing.（〜を普通にしよう）。悪いことを normalize するな、という注意にも。
nurture | v | 大切に育てる | care for something so it can grow | You have to nurture a fan base, not just sell to it. || My grandma nurtured my love of music. | music, business | foster とほぼ同じだが、より温かく、親が子を育てるようなイメージ。
obsess | v | 夢中になる・執着する | think about something all the time | I'm obsessing over this one snare sound. || Stop obsessing about what she meant by that emoji. | music, crush | obsess over / about ~。I'm obsessed with ~（〜にハマってる）は前向きにも使う。
outgrow | v | （成長して）合わなくなる・卒業する | grow too big or mature for something | We've outgrown this tiny studio. || I think I've outgrown that friend group. | music, friends | 服・場所・人間関係・習慣に使える。ネガティブではなく「成長の結果」のニュアンス。
overdo | v | やりすぎる | do too much of something | Don't overdo it on your first day back at the gym. || I overdid the salt, sorry. | gym, food | Don't overdo it. は気遣いの決まり文句。過去形は overdid。it を落とさないこと。
overshare | v | （個人的なことを）話しすぎる | tell people too much personal information | Sorry, I overshare when I'm tired. || He overshared on the first date, like, his whole medical history. | crush, online | TMI と同じ感覚。SNS 時代の新しめの単語で、自虐でよく使う。
persevere | v | 粘り強く頑張り続ける | keep trying even when it's hard | If you persevere through the first awkward months, it gets easier. || Our pastor talked about persevering through hard seasons. | faith, study | keep going の硬い版。教会・スピーチ・スポーツで多い。名詞 perseverance。
pinpoint | v | 正確に特定する | find or describe something exactly | I can't pinpoint why, but this mix sounds off. || We finally pinpointed the problem: the fridge was set too cold. | work, music | find より「ピンポイントで」。I can't pinpoint it. は言葉にできない違和感に便利。
plateau | v | 伸び悩む・頭打ちになる | stop improving after a period of progress | My bench press has plateaued for two months. || Sales plateaued after the opening rush. | gym, business | ジム・勉強・売上で頻出。名詞の hit a plateau（壁にぶつかる）も同じ意味。
provoke | v | 挑発する・引き起こす | cause a reaction, often anger | He's just trying to provoke you, don't reply. || The video provoked a ton of debate online. | conflict, online | provoke a reaction / anger。人に使えば「わざと怒らせる」。
