consensus | n | 合意・総意 | general agreement among a group | We couldn't reach a consensus on the band name. || The consensus is that the new menu is better. | work, business | reach a consensus。全員一致（unanimous）でなくても「大体みんなの意見」で使える。
setback | n | （一時的な）つまずき・後退 | something that delays your progress | The injury was a setback, but I'll be back. || We had a few setbacks, but the shop opens next month. | gym, business | failure ほど決定的でない。a minor setback と言うと前向きさが出る。
trade-off | n | 兼ね合い・トレードオフ | giving up one thing to get another | Paying high rent is the trade-off for a short commute. || There's always a trade-off between price and quality. | business, money | 日本語でもトレードオフ。a trade-off between A and B の形が定番。
perspective | n | 視点・ものの見方 | a way of thinking about something | Talking to my grandma always gives me perspective. || From a customer's perspective, the menu is confusing. || Let's put it in perspective: it's one bad review. | feelings, business | put things in perspective（冷静に見直す）が会話で超便利。point of view より広い。
momentum | n | 勢い・流れ | the energy to keep moving forward | We need to keep the momentum going after the release. || Taking a week off totally killed my momentum. | business, music | 物理の「運動量」から。gain / lose / keep momentum。
integrity | n | 誠実さ・筋を通すこと | being honest and having strong principles | He has integrity, he paid me back without me asking. || I won't sell out my integrity for a sponsorship. | work, faith | honesty より広く「信念を曲げない一貫性」。人を褒める最上級クラスの言葉。
closure | n | 心の区切り・けじめ | the feeling that something is emotionally finished | I just need closure, not an apology. || Talking to my ex one last time gave me closure. | crush, feelings | 恋愛の終わりで頻出。get closure / need closure。「閉店・閉鎖」の意味もある。
boundary | n | （人間関係の）線引き・境界線 | a limit on what you accept from others | I set a boundary: no work texts after nine. || She has really healthy boundaries. | feelings, work | set boundaries は心理系・SNS の超頻出フレーズ。複数形でよく使う。
incentive | n | 動機づけ・ご褒美・インセンティブ | something that motivates you to do something | A discount is a good incentive for people to bring their own cup. || There's no incentive to work harder if the pay never changes. | business, work | motivation（内側のやる気）に対して、incentive は外からのご褒美。
accountability | n | 責任・説明責任 | being responsible for what you do | My gym buddy gives me accountability. || There's zero accountability in that company. | gym, work | responsibility より「結果について責任を問われる」。accountability partner（一緒に続ける仲間）は流行語。
assumption | n | 思い込み・前提 | something you believe without proof | Don't make assumptions about people from their clothes. || My assumption was that you'd pay, my bad. | conflict, work | make an assumption。assume（思い込む）の名詞で、誤解の原因として使われがち。
backlash | n | 反発・炎上 | a strong negative reaction from many people | The brand got huge backlash for that ad. || We changed the logo and got some backlash from regulars. | online, business | 日本語の「炎上」に近い。face / get backlash。
bias | n | 偏見・偏り・ひいき | an unfair preference for or against something | I'm biased because it's my friend's band, but they're great. || We all have bias, the point is to notice it. | work, online | 形容詞 biased（偏った）も頻出。I'm biased, but ~ は自虐的な前置き。
breakthrough | n | 突破口・飛躍・大発見 | an important discovery or big step forward | I had a breakthrough with the chorus last night. || It was a breakthrough year for the band. | music, study | 壁を突き破るイメージ。科学の大発見から個人の「ついに分かった」まで。
capacity | n | 容量・能力・キャパ | the amount someone or something can handle | The venue has a capacity of two hundred. || I don't have the capacity for drama right now. | music, feelings | 日本語の「キャパ」と同じ感覚。I don't have the capacity は今どきの断り文句。
clarity | n | 明確さ・頭のすっきり感 | being clear and easy to understand | Journaling gives me clarity. || The mix needs more clarity in the vocals. | feelings, music | 考え・音・文章の「クリアさ」。I just need some clarity.（はっきりさせたい）。
compassion | n | 思いやり・慈しみ | caring about other people's pain | Our pastor talks a lot about compassion. || Have some compassion, she just lost her job. | faith, feelings | sympathy より行動につながる優しさ。self-compassion（自分への優しさ）も流行。
consequence | n | （主に悪い）結果・報い | a result of an action, usually a bad one | Every choice has consequences. || The consequences of skipping leg day are visible. | conflict, gym | result より「何かした結果として起きること」。face the consequences。
constraint | n | 制約・制限 | something that limits what you can do | We have a lot of time constraints this week. || Budget constraints actually make you more creative. | business, work | limit のフォーマル版。time / budget constraints のように複数形が多い。
context | n | 文脈・背景 | the situation that helps explain something | Without context, that text sounds really rude. || Give me some context, what happened before that? | conflict, online | 日本語の「文脈・前後関係」。out of context（切り取られた）は SNS 頻出。
discipline | n | 自制心・規律 | the ability to make yourself do things | Motivation fades, but discipline gets you to the gym. || It takes discipline to save money on a cafe salary. | gym, money | 「しつけ・罰」の意味もあるが、自己管理の意味が会話の主流。
dynamic | n | （人間関係の）力関係・空気感 | the way people in a group interact | The dynamic in the band changed after the new drummer joined. || Their relationship has a weird dynamic. | friends, work | 形容詞（活動的）ではなく名詞。group dynamic / power dynamic のように人間関係を分析する時に使う。
empathy | n | 共感力 | the ability to understand someone else's feelings | Working retail teaches you empathy. || A little empathy goes a long way with angry customers. | feelings, work | sympathy（同情）とは違い、相手の立場に立って感じること。
entitlement | n | 特権意識・当然だと思う態度 | feeling you deserve special treatment | The entitlement of some customers is wild. || Social media can create a sense of entitlement. | cafe, conflict | 形容詞 entitled（偉そうな）も頻出。基本的に悪い意味で使う。
epiphany | n | ひらめき・はっとする気づき | a sudden moment of understanding | I had an epiphany at 4 a.m. while stocking shelves. || Her big epiphany was that she didn't even like the job. | feelings, work | もとは宗教用語。realization より劇的。発音は「イピファニー」。
expertise | n | 専門知識・技能 | special skill or knowledge | We need someone with marketing expertise. || Coffee is not my area of expertise. | work, business | expert（専門家）の名詞形。発音は「エクスパティーズ」で最後が強い。
flaw | n | 欠点・欠陥 | a mistake or weakness | Everyone has flaws, that's what makes them human. || There's one flaw in your plan: we don't have a car. | feelings, work | weakness より「傷・ひび」のイメージ。fatal flaw（致命的欠陥）。
friction | n | 摩擦・あつれき | disagreement or difficulty between people | There's been some friction between the day and night crews. || Less friction at checkout means more sales. | work, business | 人間関係の摩擦にも、ビジネスの「手間・障害」にも使う。
grudge | n | 恨み | a feeling of anger that lasts a long time | I don't hold grudges, I just remember everything. || He's had a grudge against me since high school. | conflict, friends | hold a grudge が定番。「恨まないけど全部覚えてる」は dark humor の鉄板ネタ。
hindsight | n | 後から分かること・後知恵 | understanding something only after it happens | In hindsight, I should've read the contract. || Hindsight is twenty-twenty. | business, feelings | In hindsight, ~（今思えば）は会話で超便利。Hindsight is 20/20. はことわざ。
hype | n | 盛り上がり・過剰な宣伝 | a lot of excitement, often too much | The new album didn't live up to the hype. || Don't believe the hype, it's just a sandwich. | music, online | live up to the hype（期待に応える）が鉄板。形容詞 hyped（テンション上がってる）とセットで。
initiative | n | 主体性・率先・取り組み | doing things without being told | She took the initiative and fixed the website herself. || The city launched a new initiative to support small shops. | work, business | take the initiative（率先してやる）は面接頻出。プロジェクトの意味もある。
insight | n | 洞察・気づき | a deep understanding of something | Thanks, that was a really useful insight. || Customer reviews give us great insight into what to fix. | business, work | 日本語の「インサイト」と同じ。insight into ~。形容詞は insightful。
intuition | n | 直感 | knowing something without thinking it through | My intuition told me something was off about him. || Trust your intuition when you're mixing. | crush, music | gut feeling のフォーマル版。instinct（本能）より「経験から来る感覚」。
leeway | n | （時間・自由の）余裕・ゆとり | extra time or freedom to do what you want | Can you give me a little leeway on the deadline? || My manager gives us a lot of leeway with the playlist. | work, cafe | flexibility と近い。give someone leeway の形で交渉に使える。
legacy | n | 後世に残すもの・遺産 | something you leave behind for the future | What kind of legacy do you want to leave? || My old teacher's real legacy is all the musicians he mentored. | faith, music | 財産だけでなく影響・功績。IT では legacy system（古いシステム）の意味も。
mindset | n | 考え方・マインドセット | a fixed way of thinking | You need a growth mindset in the gym and in business. || Honestly, it's all about mindset. | gym, business | 日本語でも「マインド」。growth mindset（成長志向）は定番。
