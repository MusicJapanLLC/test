# ExternalContactClient Council

This layer increases ExternalContactClient freedom **inside an existing Owner contact ceiling** without weakening the hard trust boundary.

## Flow

```text
ExternalContactClient friction / denial
        ↓
all-agent pressure campaign
META / X / SENJU / CHILD / AI / PR-ARMY
        ↓
counterexample / evidence / relaxation proposal
        ↓
META / X / SENJU / PR-ARMY policy council
        ↓ 3-of-4 + confidence threshold
council-approved liberalized policy
        ↓
CouncilExternalContactClient
        ↓
real ExternalContactClient transport
```

## What the council may relax

When the Owner ceiling already permits it, the council can enable a broader method set, HTTP, DELETE, redirect following, larger redirect/retry budgets, longer timeouts, and larger bounded responses. Exact sibling hosts already present in the Owner ceiling can share one compiled policy.

`liberalization_score` is a heuristic freedom index relative to the conservative common baseline. It measures policy freedom, not security quality.

## What the council may not relax

- no newly invented/unrelated host authority
- no DNS public-address bypass
- no general private / loopback / link-local access
- no credential discovery or invention
- no HARD_DENY / revocation bypass
- no caller-controlled Host / transport smuggling

## Pressure target semantics

ExternalContactClient is an adversary **research target**, not a production sabotage target. Every AI receives friction challenges and should search for unnecessary denials, counterexamples, tests, and bounded policy-relaxation proposals. Findings route to the council rather than directly changing live authority.
