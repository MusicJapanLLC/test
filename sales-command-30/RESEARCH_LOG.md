# Revenue Recovery AI — Research Log

## 2026-08-29 — Product thesis reset

### What the market evidence changed
- Generic CRM dashboards are crowded and weakly differentiated.
- The stronger recurring pain is not contact storage; it is missed follow-up, stale opportunities, context loss, and founder memory becoming the execution layer.
- Small teams value systems that tell them who to contact now, preserve context, and remove manual CRM work.
- A useful wedge is revenue already paid to acquire: old leads, proposals, warm referrals, stalled opportunities, timing objections, and no-shows.
- Product value must be measured in recovered pipeline / recovered conversations / reduced leakage, not number of automations or AI features.

### Current product wedge
**Revenue Recovery AI** — find recoverable stale opportunities, rank them by likely value, generate a context-aware next action and follow-up draft, record the outcome, and learn from successful recoveries.

### Design rules learned from research
1. Daily action queue > generic dashboard.
2. Fewer fields and fewer stages > enterprise CRM imitation.
3. Next action + date are load-bearing fields.
4. Automate repeatable logistics; keep human judgment for actual selling.
5. Success/failure outcomes must feed future prioritization.
6. Successful recovery copy should become evidence for future drafts.
7. Existing Gmail/Calendar/CRM context should update the relationship automatically where reliable.
8. Do not claim recovered revenue without verified commercial outcome.
9. Every product experiment should have a hypothesis and KEEP/REVERT decision.
10. Continuous market research and product experiments are separate loops.

### Current known blocker
Make organization/team remains paused because operations or data-transfer quota is exceeded. Do not burn retries while paused. Continue app, GitHub, research, and safe product work.

### Next evidence questions
- Which vertical has the highest willingness to pay for stale-pipeline recovery in Japan?
- Which recovery trigger creates the clearest ROI proof: proposal silence, missed inquiry, old lead, timing objection, or no-show?
- What minimum import/connect workflow gets a customer to first recovered opportunity in under 10 minutes?
- Which outcome metric is strongest for pricing: recovered pipeline yen, reactivated conversations, meetings booked, or time saved?
